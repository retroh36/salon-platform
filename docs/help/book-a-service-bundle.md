Book a bundle (like Highlight, Gloss and Cut) in one step: its services go in one after another, with the gaps between them free for the stylist.

1. Tap an empty time in the stylist's column on the calendar.
2. Choose the client.
3. Under **Service**, choose the bundle from **Bundles** at the top of the list. Its services fill in as Service 1, 2, 3, each with its start time, and the gap between them shows between the services.
4. Change anything for one part: another stylist (an assistant for the gloss), its start time (a longer or shorter gap), its chair, or another service. **Remove** takes a part off.
5. Tap **Create booking**.

## Good to know

- Another client can be booked with the stylist in each gap, and while any service is processing.
- Dragging one part on the calendar moves the whole bundle by the same time. Parts with the same stylist move to the new column too, and parts with someone else stay with them.
- To change one part later, open the visit and tap **Edit booking**. The other parts stay where they are.
- At checkout each service is its own line, with its own price, stylist, commission and tip.
- Booking details says "Booked as" and the bundle's name, and the calendar block's tooltip lists every part.
- Clients can book a bundle on the booking site too, when **Clients can book it online** is ticked and each of its services can be booked online. It shows under **Bundles** at the top of the services, is booked whole with one stylist, and keeps its gaps. Rescheduling it from the client's link keeps the gaps as booked.
