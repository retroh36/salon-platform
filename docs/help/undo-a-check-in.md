Take back a check-in made by mistake, so the visit is booked again.

1. Right after checking in, tap **Undo** on the message at the bottom of the screen (it stays for 10 seconds).
2. Later on, tap the visit on the calendar to open booking details.
3. Tap **⋯** (More actions) at the bottom, then **Undo check-in**.

## Good to know

- The visit goes back to booked, and any service times the stylist tapped since are cleared.
- It's kept in the visit's history (the **History** tab) with your name.
- After checkout there's no undo: a manager or owner voids the sale instead. Booking details links to it ("view the sale").
