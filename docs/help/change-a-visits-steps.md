Change the steps of a service built from steps for one visit only (a longer processing, a step added or taken out), without changing the service on the menu.

1. On the calendar, tap the visit to open booking details.
2. Under the service, each step shows with its time. Tap **Edit steps**.
3. Change a step's minutes or name, tick or untick **Processing**, or use **Add a step**, **Add processing**, the arrows and **×**.
4. Tap **Save steps**.

## Good to know

- The visit's end moves with the new length, and the calendar block shows the new processing times at once.
- The new steps are checked like a booking: if a busy step would land on another client, blocked time or outside the stylist's hours, it isn't saved and says why.
- Only this visit changes. To change the service for every new booking, edit it on Services (see "Build a service from steps").
- A visit that's checked out, canceled or a no-show can't be changed.
