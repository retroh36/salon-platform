Ask Denise about the day, availability, clients or the salon, in your own words.

1. Go to **Calendar** (or **Dashboard**) and type your question in the **Ask Denise** bar at the top, for example "Find a haircut with Gil after 1 PM".
2. Tap the send button (**Ask**). Her panel opens and she shows what she's doing while she works.
3. Read her answer. Ask a follow-up in the **Ask Denise…** box at the bottom of her panel.
4. If she suggests a booking, find the client in the card, then tap **Book it** (or **Dismiss**).

## Good to know

- She can find openings, look up a client's habits, who's overdue, how long stylists take, the day's schedule, stock and (for owners and managers) sales.
- Her numbers come from the salon's own records; she never makes them up.
- She takes one question at a time. Nothing changes until someone approves it.
- Stylists see only their own figures and no money.
- If Denise isn't connected, or the salon's daily limit is reached, she says so and the rest of the app works as usual.
