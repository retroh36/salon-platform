Ask Denise about the day, availability, clients or the salon, in your own words, from any page.

1. Tap the Denise button in the bottom right corner (when the page has a button there, it shrinks to a small tab at the edge), or **Ask Denise** at the bottom of the sidebar, or press **⌘K** or **Ctrl+K** from anywhere. On the calendar, use the **Denise** tab in the side panel; at checkout, the Denise button at the top.
2. Type your question in the message box at the bottom, for example "Who's free for a color at 3?", and send it. The box shows an example to start from (on the calendar, one naming someone on your team). While Denise works, the chat shows what's happening.
3. Read Denise's answer, and ask a follow-up in the same box.
4. If Denise suggests a booking, find the client in the card, then tap **Book it** (or **Dismiss**).

## Good to know

- The conversation stays when you go to another page, so you can carry on where you left off.
- On the calendar and on Scheduling, Denise opens as a small card in the bottom right corner, so you still see the whole team. It grows as the conversation does. Tap **–** (Minimize Denise) to shrink it to a small bar showing how many things are waiting, and tap the bar to bring it back; **×** closes it. Either way the conversation stays, and each page remembers how you left it. On other pages the chat opens beside the page.
- Denise knows which page you're on: on a client's profile, at checkout or with a visit open, "When's their next visit?" means that client.
- Denise can find openings, look up a client's habits, who's overdue, how long stylists take, the day's schedule, stock, events and (for owners and managers) sales. The numbers come from the salon's own records.
- Nothing changes until someone approves it. Stylists see only their own figures and no money.
- If Denise isn't connected, or the salon's daily limit is reached, the chat says so and the rest of the app works as usual.
- After 8 seconds Denise shows **Still thinking…**. If a question takes over 25 seconds, Denise stops and says so: tap **Try again**, or ask it a shorter way.
