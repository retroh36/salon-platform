With many stylists, the calendar keeps each column wide enough to read and scrolls sideways. Or squeeze everyone onto one screen.

1. Go to **Calendar**.
2. To move across the stylists, scroll sideways: swipe left or right on an iPad, or use a trackpad or Shift + mouse wheel. The times on the left and the stylists' names at the top stay in place.
3. To see everyone at once, choose **Compact** zoom, then tap **Fit all**.
4. Names shorten to fit (to initials if they must) and each visit shows the client's name; hover over or tap a visit for the details.
5. Tap **Fit all** again, or choose another zoom, to go back to readable columns.

## Good to know

- **Fit all** is only in **Compact** zoom, and is remembered on this device.
- Dragging a visit near the left or right edge scrolls the calendar so you can drop it on someone further along.
- To show fewer people instead, use a saved view. See Choose who shows on the calendar with a saved view.
