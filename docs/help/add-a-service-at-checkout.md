Add a service that wasn't booked, such as a gloss or a treatment done on the spot.

1. Open the checkout (tap the visit, then **Check out**, or start a **New sale**).
2. Under the lines, tap **Add service**.
3. Choose the **Stylist** who did it.
4. Type in **Find a service** (for example "Gloss") and tap the service.
5. The line appears under that stylist, marked "added at checkout". Tap it to change the price or **Quantity** if needed.

## Good to know

- The price is that stylist's price for the service (their own, or their level's).
- Only services the chosen stylist offers are listed.
- Added services count toward the stylist's commission and their tip suggestions.

## If it doesn't work

- "This stylist doesn't offer a service like that.": pick another stylist or check the spelling.
