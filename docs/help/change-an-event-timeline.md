Move someone's hair or makeup to another time or another artist, with everything checked as you go.

1. Go to **Events**, tap the event and tap **Edit event**.
2. Under **Timeline**, find the person's bar on their artist's row.
3. Drag the bar left or right to change the time, or up or down onto another artist doing the same work. You can also tap a bar and use the arrow keys.
4. Read any message in red: two people with one artist, someone in two chairs, or someone not ready in time. Move the bar again until it's gone.
5. Check each artist under **Timeline**: "has clients booked" or "has time off" means they aren't free for their new times.
6. Tap **Save changes**.

## Good to know

- Once an event is booked, each person on its team has one block on the calendar for it, from leaving the salon (on location) to getting back. Clients can't be booked into it.
- A booked event can't be saved with someone who isn't free then. Choose someone else or move the timeline.
- **Work it out again** plans the whole day again from the ready-by time.
- If someone on the team gets booked before the client agrees, they come off the event when it's booked, and the front desk is told to choose someone else.

## If it doesn't work

- **Save changes** is greyed out: sort out the message under the timeline first.
- The block on the calendar can't be moved there: change the event instead.
