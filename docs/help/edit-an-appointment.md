Change a visit's services, stylist, start time, chair, client or notes.

1. On the calendar, tap the visit to open booking details.
2. Tap **⋯** (More actions) at the bottom, then **Edit booking**.
3. Change what you need: the **Service**, **Stylist**, **Starts** time or **Chair** on each service, **Add another service**, or **Remove** one. Tap **Change client** to pick someone else.
4. If the visit is part of a repeating series, choose under **Change**: **This visit only**, **This and following visits** or **All visits in the series**.
5. Tap **Save changes**.

## Good to know

- You can edit a visit until it's checked out. Checked-out, canceled and no-show visits can't be changed.
- The date can't be changed here. To move a visit to another day, cancel it (reason **Client rescheduled**) and book the new day.
- For a series, each visit is checked on its own; any that don't fit are listed so you can sort them out.
- To just move a visit to a new time or stylist, you can drag it instead (see Move an appointment).

## If it doesn't work

- The change is refused: the reason shows on the service, with Denise's nearest times that work. Tap one to use it.
