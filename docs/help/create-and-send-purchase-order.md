Put together an order for a supplier and mark it as sent.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Inventory**, then **Orders**, then **New order**.
2. Choose the **Supplier**, and add **Notes** if you like.
3. Under **Products**, choose a product, then enter the quantity and **Cost each ($)**.
4. Tap **Add a product** for each more line. Tap the × to remove one.
5. Tap **Create draft**.
6. Place the order with the supplier your usual way (email, phone or their website).
7. Back on the order, tap **Mark as sent**.

## Good to know

- Maestra doesn't send the order to the supplier for you; **Mark as sent** only records that it's been placed.
- A draft can be changed (**Save draft**) or deleted (**Delete draft**) until it's sent.
- When the delivery arrives, see "Receive an order".
- Denise can draft orders for you. See "Review Denise's reorder drafts".
