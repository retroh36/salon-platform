Change a service's name, price, timing or who offers it, or take it off the menu.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Services**.
2. Tap the service.
3. Change what you need: name, category, **Default price ($)**, **Prices by level**, **Time** or **Who offers this**.
4. Tap **Save changes**.
5. To take it off the menu, scroll to the bottom and tap **Archive service**.
6. To bring an archived service back, open **Archived** at the bottom of the Services list, tap the service, then **Restore service**.

## Good to know

- Archived services can't be booked. Past appointments keep them.
- An archived service can't be edited until it's restored.
- Front desk and stylists can see services but can't change them.
- To change how long one stylist takes for a service, see "Use Denise's suggested service length".
