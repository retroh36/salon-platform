Owners set goals for any stylist, and owners and managers see how everyone is pacing.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Team**, then the **Goals** tab.
2. Each stylist's goals show their progress, what they need per working day and whether they're **Ahead**, **On pace** or **Behind**.
3. To set one, tap **Set a goal**, choose who it's **For**, the **Goal** and **Each** period, enter the **Target** and tap **Save goal**.
4. To remove one, tap **Remove** next to it.

## Good to know

- Only owners set or remove goals here. Managers can see them.
- Stylists can set their own goals on My day, but they can't change one an owner set.
- Goals can be service revenue, retail sales, rebooking %, new clients or average ticket, for a week, month, quarter or year.
- Sales count before tax and after discounts, and refunds come off.
