Automations do the routine steps for every event: when something happens, they send the client your message, add a to-do for the team, or move the event to one of your own stages.

1. Go to **Settings** and open **Bridal and events**.
2. Under **Automations**, give the new one a name.
3. Choose **When**: an inquiry comes in, the proposal is signed, the deposit is paid, or so many days before the event. Set how many days to wait.
4. Choose **Then**: send the client a message, add a to-do, or move the event to a stage.
5. Tick **On** and tap **Add automation**.

## Good to know

- A message to clients goes out only once an owner turns it on. If anyone changes its words or when it goes, it waits for an owner again.
- Messages are your own words, with the client's first name, the salon, the event and its date filled in. They go on the channel the client allows, between 9am and 8pm.
- To-dos show on the event under **To do**. Tick one off when it's done.
- Nothing runs for an event that's lost or completed, and each automation runs once for each event.
- What each automation did is in the event's **History**.
