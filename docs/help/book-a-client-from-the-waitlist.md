When time opens up, Denise suggests waitlist clients who fit it. You choose who to book, or offer the time to someone with one tap.

1. After a cancellation, no-show or fix frees some time, open Denise's card (tap her face under the calendar, or her small bar in the corner if she's minimized).
2. Find the waitlist card. It shows the opening and the clients who fit, best first: those who asked for that stylist, then whoever has waited longest.
3. To book someone after you've spoken to them, tap **Book** next to their name. The visit is booked and the client comes off the waitlist.
4. To let them choose, tap **Offer** instead. They get the salon's message with a link, and the row says **Offered · until** the time it's held to.
5. The client taps **Take it** or **No thanks** on their phone. If the cancellation policy needs a card for that visit (or a deposit), the page shows the policy first: they agree and add a card (or use the one on file), and any deposit is paid before it's booked. If their card doesn't go through, nothing is booked and they stay on the waitlist. Either way you get a notice on the Dashboard's For you card, and a visit they take is on the calendar.

## Good to know

- An offer is held for the client for an hour, and never later than 15 minutes before the time. The time itself isn't held: if someone else books it first, the client is told it's gone and stays on the waitlist.
- Offering again, or offering someone a different time, replaces their earlier offer.
- The offer goes on the channel the client gets visit messages on. If they don't get any, call them instead.
- If the cancellation policy needs a card or a deposit for the visit, the confirmation says so (for a time they take from an offer, booking details does): send the link from booking details.
- Denise never contacts anyone. Only you send an offer, and the message is the salon's fixed wording.
- Each match shows the services and the time it would be.
- To add someone to the list, see Add a client to the waitlist.
