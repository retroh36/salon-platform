Add someone new, with their role, what they do and when they work, and invite them to sign in.

1. In the sidebar, under **Salon**, tap **Team**, then **Add team member**.
2. Fill in their **First name**, **Last name**, **Email** (the one they'll sign in with) and **Phone**.
3. Choose their **Role**, and for a stylist their **Level**.
4. Under **Services they do**, tick each service they can be booked for (**Tick all** to start from everything).
5. Under **Working hours**, check each day. It starts from the salon's opening hours; clear a day they don't work.
6. Leave **Email them an invite to sign in** ticked, then tap **Add to the team**.

## Good to know

- Managers can add anyone but an owner; only owners make someone an owner.
- They choose their own password from the invite email. Until they do, they show as **Invited**.
- Pay rates, a photo, split shifts, one-off changes and time off are on **Schedule** (the member's page links there).
- To change someone later, tap their name on the Team directory.

## If it doesn't work

- "That email already has a Maestra login": use a different email for them.
