What a client sees on your booking site, so you can walk them through it on the phone.

1. They open your booking link and choose up to 4 services (each shows its price range), then tap **Continue**.
2. They pick a stylist, or **No preference** for the first stylist free at their time.
3. They pick a date and time. If a day says **No times this day**, they try another date.
4. Under **Your details**, they choose **Text me** or **Email me**, enter their number or email and tap **Send code**.
5. They type the 6-digit code and tap **Confirm**. Clients on file see **Welcome back**; new clients add their name, phone and email.
6. On **Review and book**, they read the cancellation policy, add a card if the salon asks for one, tick **I agree to the cancellation policy** and tap **Book appointment** (or **Pay … deposit and book**).
7. They see **You're booked**, with buttons to change or cancel.

## Good to know

- The code expires in 10 minutes; they can tap **Send a new code**.
- Texts and email aren't connected yet, so codes and confirmations don't reach clients' phones for now. Ask an owner before promising a client a code by text.
- New clients can book only if the salon allows it; otherwise they're asked to call.
- Deposits are paid online when they book, with test cards for now (no real money moves yet).
- To change or cancel, see Help a client change or cancel an online booking.
