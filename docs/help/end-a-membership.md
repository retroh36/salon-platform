End a client's membership when they ask, by the plan's rules.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Clients** and open the client.
2. Find their membership under **Membership**.
3. Tap **End membership**.
4. Read the date it can end on: the end of a paid period, after any minimum term and notice.
5. Enter **Why**.
6. Owners and managers only: to end it today instead, tick **End it today instead (an exception to the rules)**.
7. Tap **End on** (the date) or **End it now**.

## Good to know

- They keep member benefits until the end date, and there are no more payments.
- Changed their mind before the end date? Tap **Keep membership** and it renews as usual.
- A membership whose payment failed ends right away.
- A membership also ends by itself after its fourth failed payment.
