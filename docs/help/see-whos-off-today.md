Stylists who aren't working that day are left off the calendar, so it only shows the people who are working. You can still see who they are and why.

1. Go to **Calendar** and open the day.
2. Tap the chip next to the department menu, for example **10 off today**.
3. The list shows each person who's off and why: **Day off**, **Sick**, **Vacation**, **Personal** (with any note), or **Salon closed**.
4. To put them back on the calendar, tick **Show them on the calendar anyway**. Their whole day shows greyed out. Untick it to hide them again.

## Good to know

- Someone off for only part of the day still shows, with those hours greyed out and labeled (for example **Time off: Personal · Dentist**).
- Anyone with a booking that day always shows, even if they're off, so nothing is out of sight.
- When nobody is working (for example the salon is closed), the calendar says so. Tap **Show them anyway** to see everyone.
- Time off is added on Team → Schedule. See Request time off or Approve time off.
