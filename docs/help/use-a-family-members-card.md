When a client has no card on file (often a child), a family member can let their own card on file hold the client's bookings. The card's owner approves it by link.

1. Link them as a family first (the **Family** card on the client's profile).
2. Go to the client's profile and find the **Card on file** card.
3. Tap **Use Jen's card…** (the family member's name). With more than one, choose **Whose card**.
4. Check the mobile number or email, then tap **Ask Jen**. They get a text or email with a secure link.
5. The card shows **Waiting for Jen to approve their card** until they answer.
6. On the link, they read the cancellation policy for the client's visits, type their full name, tick the box and tap **Approve** (or **Decline**).
7. Once approved, the client's **Card on file** shows the card with the family member's name, and **Approved by** with their name and the date.

## Good to know

- The card holds the client's bookings and is charged under the cancellation policy for the client's visits, like any card on file. Nothing is charged when it's approved.
- What the family member agreed to is kept word for word, with their typed name.
- **Stop using this card** takes it off the client. The family member's own card stays.
- It also stops by itself if the family member's card is removed or replaced, or the client adds a card of their own. Ask again for the new card.
- **Cancel the request** stops a link that hasn't been answered.
- The family member needs a card of their own on file first. If they don't have one, send them a card link from their profile.
- Texts and email wait in the outbox until the salon goes live with messages.

## If it doesn't work

- No **Use … card** button: they aren't in a family yet, nobody else in the family has a card of their own, or the client already has a card.
- "The card on file has changed since this was sent": the family member's card was replaced. Ask again.
