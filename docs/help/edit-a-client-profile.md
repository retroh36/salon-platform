Update a client's contact details, allergies, preferences and notes.

1. Go to **Clients** and search by name or phone (or use the search bar at the top of any page).
2. Tap the client to open their profile.
3. Scroll down to **Details**.
4. Change what you need under **Contact** and **Care**.
5. Tap **Save changes**.

## Good to know

- Only tick **Texts OK** or **Marketing emails OK** if the client agreed.
- Stylists can see client details but can't change them.
- From booking details, tap the client's name to jump to their profile.
- The profile also holds patch tests, signed forms, color formulas, photos, packages and memberships.

## If it doesn't work

- "Fix the highlighted fields": the message under a field says what to fix (for example, a phone number or email that doesn't look right).
