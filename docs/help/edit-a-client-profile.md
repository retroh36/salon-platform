Update a client's contact details, birthday, allergies, preferred stylist and preferences.

1. Go to **Clients** and search by name or phone (or use the search bar at the top of the Calendar).
2. Tap the client to open their profile.
3. Tap **Edit details** under their name.
4. Change what you need under **Contact** and **Care**.
5. Tap **Save changes**. You're back on their profile.

## Good to know

- Notes aren't in this form: they're the big box near the top of the profile (see Keep notes on a client).
- Only tick **Offers by text OK** or **Offers by email OK** if the client agreed.
- Allergies show in a red box at the top of their profile, in booking details and in the visit's details on the calendar.
- Stylists can see client details but can't change them.
- From booking details, tap the client's name to jump to their profile.

## If it doesn't work

- "Fix the highlighted fields": the message under a field says what to fix (for example, a phone number or email that doesn't look right).
