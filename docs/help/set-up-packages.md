Create the packages staff can sell at checkout.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Settings**, then **Packages**.
2. Under **New package**, enter a **Name** (for example "5 blowouts") and the **Price ($)**.
3. Under **Services**, choose a service and how many. Tap **Add a service** for more.
4. In **Lasts (months)**, enter how long it lasts, or leave it empty so it never expires.
5. Under **For stylists who are**, untick any levels it shouldn't cover.
6. Tap **Add package**.
7. To change one later, tap **Edit** on it, make changes, and tap **Save package**.

## Good to know

- To stop selling a package, tap **Edit** and untick **On sale**. Clients who already have it keep it.
- Each use is valued at its share of the price, used for refunds and reports.
