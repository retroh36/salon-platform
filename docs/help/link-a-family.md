Link a parent and their children, or a couple, so you can see them together and know who usually pays. Each person stays their own client, with their own visits, formulas and history.

1. Go to **Clients** and tap the name of someone in the family.
2. Find the **Family** card on the right of their profile.
3. Tap **Add family** in the card's top right corner (**Edit** once they're in one).
4. Check the **Family name** (it starts as "The" and their last name).
5. Under **Add someone**, type a name or phone number and tap the person to add them. Add everyone in the family the same way.
6. Tick who usually pays, or **Each pays their own**.
7. Tap **Save**. The card lists everyone, each a link to their profile, with **Usually pays** by the payer.

## Good to know

- A family has 2 to 12 people, and someone can be in one family at a time.
- To take someone out, tap **Edit**, then **Remove** by their name, and **Save**. They keep their own profile.
- **Remove family** (under **Edit**) unlinks everyone. Nobody's profile or history is deleted.
- Someone new (a child who hasn't visited yet)? Add them as a client first, then link them here.
- Stylists see the family on the profiles of their own clients, but can't change it.

## If it doesn't work

- "... is already in another family": take them out of their other family first, from that family's card.
- No **Add family** button: only owners, managers and the front desk change families.
