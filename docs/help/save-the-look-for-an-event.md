Keep notes and photos of the look you agreed, so the whole team has it on the day.

1. Open the event: tap it on **Events**, or tap its block on the calendar.
2. Under **The look**, type in **Notes on the look**. It saves as you type.
3. Tap **Add photo** to take a photo or choose one.

## Good to know

- Stylists on the event can open it from their block on the calendar and add notes and photos too.
- The client's own inspiration photos, from an online inquiry, are under **Their inspiration**.
- The notes are on the day-of sheet.
- An event can have up to 12 photos of the look. Photos are private to the salon.
