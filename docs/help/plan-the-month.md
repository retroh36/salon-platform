See everyone's shifts for the whole month, plan weeks ahead and publish it in one go.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Scheduling**, then **Month** next to the date.
2. Each row is one person and each column one day: their hours ("9–5", a split shift on two lines), approved time off (hatched) and days off. A warning sign over a day means Denise spotted something to check: hover over it, or read her list above.
3. Tap a day to change that person's hours. Tap a name to see their whole month as a calendar, with their hours, days working, time off and clients booked.
4. To repeat a week, tap **Copy a week forward…**, choose the week, how many weeks after it, and everyone or one person, then **Copy into the draft**.
5. On someone's month, tap **Copy from someone…** to give them another person's shifts for the days you choose.
6. When it looks right, tap **Publish month**. Everyone whose hours changed gets a notice.

## Good to know

- Changes are a draft (dashed) until you publish: the calendar and online booking keep the published hours until then.
- Days already past are never copied over.
- A repeating pattern (every other week, say) shows as it falls on each day; change the pattern itself on the week view.
- The front desk sees the published month and can't change it.
