Give a stylist one of your salon's levels (for example Junior stylist, Artisan, Senior or Master). Their level sets which price they charge for services that have level prices.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Team**, then the **Schedule** tab.
2. Tap the stylist's name in the row of names at the top.
3. Scroll to **Level and commission**.
4. Under **Level**, choose their level or **No level**.
5. Tap **Save**.

## Good to know

- On the calendar, a level shows as a colored ring and badge on the stylist's photo and a bar along the top of their column. Tap **Key** under the calendar to see what each means.
- Your levels, their names, order, colors and badges are set on Settings → Levels. See "Set up stylist levels".
- A new level changes prices for new bookings and checkouts, not visits already booked.
- Each service's level prices are set on the service. See "Set prices by level for a service".
- A stylist's own price for a service beats their level's price. See "Set a stylist's own price for a service".
