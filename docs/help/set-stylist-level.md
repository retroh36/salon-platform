Give a stylist a level (Artisan, Senior or Master). Their level sets which price they charge for services that have level prices.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Team**, then the **Schedule** tab.
2. Tap the stylist's name in the row of names at the top.
3. Scroll to **Level and commission**.
4. Under **Level**, choose **Artisan**, **Senior**, **Master** or **No level**.
5. Tap **Save**.

## Good to know

- On the calendar, a level shows as a color and badge on the stylist's photo and column: gold for Artisan, berry for Senior, emerald for Master. Tap **Key** under the calendar to see what each means.
- A new level changes prices for new bookings and checkouts, not visits already booked.
- Each service's level prices are set on the service. See "Set prices by level for a service".
- A stylist's own price for a service beats their level's price. See "Set a stylist's own price for a service".
