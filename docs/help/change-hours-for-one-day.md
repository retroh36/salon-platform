Give a team member different hours on one date, such as a late shift or an extra day, without changing their weekly hours.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Team**, then the **Schedule** tab.
2. Tap the person's name in the row of names at the top.
3. Scroll to **One-off changes**.
4. Choose the **Date**.
5. Set **From** and **To**. For a split day, tap **Add split** and set the second part.
6. Tap **Save change**.

## Good to know

- The change replaces their weekly hours for that date only. It shows as **Changed hours** in the week view.
- To undo a change, tap **Remove** next to it in the list.
- For a day off, use time off instead. See "Request time off".
