When someone in the client's family pays for their visit, name them on the checkout. The receipt goes to them, and so do the points the sale earns, unless your salon gives them to the client who had the visit.

1. Open the checkout for the visit (**Check out** in booking details), or start a new sale for the client.
2. Under the client's name, find **Paid by**. If the family has someone who usually pays, they're already chosen.
3. To change it, choose someone else in the family from **Paid by**, or the client themselves.
4. Take payment as usual and tap **Complete sale**.
5. Tap **Email** under **Receipt**: the payer's email is filled in. Check it and tap **Send**.

## Good to know

- **Paid by** shows only when the client is linked to a family (see Link a family).
- The points badge says whose points the sale uses. Rewards taken on this sale come from those points; changing who pays takes any chosen rewards back off.
- Bonuses for the visit itself (first visit, referral, rebooking) always go to the client.
- A payment link goes to the payer's phone or email.
- The receipt and the sale say "paid by" with the payer's name.

## If it doesn't work

- "Only someone in the client's family can pay for them": they were taken out of the family. Choose someone who's still in it, or the client.
