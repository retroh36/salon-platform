Clients get a reminder before each visit, in fixed words filled in for the visit. Choose whether they're sent and how long before.

1. Go to Settings, then **Messages**.
2. Under **Appointment reminders**, tick or untick **Send clients a reminder before each visit**.
3. Under **When**, choose how long before the visit: 2, 4 or 12 hours, a day, two days or three days.

## Good to know

- The card shows the reminder as a client reads it. When the salon takes bookings online, it has a link for the client to view, change or cancel their visit (up to the salon's cutoff); otherwise it gives the salon's phone number.
- It goes by text or email, whichever the client gets messages about their visits on. A client who replied STOP to texts gets it by email; a client with neither gets none.
- One reminder per visit. A visit moved to another time gets one for its new time. Visits booked in the last hour, and visits starting within the hour, get none.
- Reminders go between 9am and 8pm at the salon. One due overnight goes at 9am, as long as the visit is more than an hour away.
- Until the salon goes live with texts and email, reminders wait in the outbox and nothing reaches a client (see "Go live with texts and email").
- Booking details' **History** tab shows each reminder written for the visit.
- Denise never writes or sends reminders.
