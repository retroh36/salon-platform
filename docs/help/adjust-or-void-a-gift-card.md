Correct a gift card's balance, or stop a card working.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Gift cards**.
2. Under **Check a balance**, scan or type the code and tap **Check balance** (or tap the card in the list).
3. To change the balance, go to **Adjust balance**: choose **Add** or **Take off** under **Change**, enter the **Amount ($)** and a **Reason**, then tap **Adjust**.
4. To stop the card, go to **Void**: enter **Why it's being voided**, then tap **Void card**.
5. Check **History** on the card: the change is listed with your name and reason.

## Good to know

- Voiding takes the whole balance off and the card stops working. It can't be undone.
- Adjust and Void only show for active cards.
- To undo a gift card sale itself, void or refund the sale instead (see Refund a sale).
