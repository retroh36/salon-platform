Clients can send an event inquiry from the booking site ("Planning a wedding or event?"). It comes in as an Inquiry for you to plan.

1. Go to **Events**. New inquiries are under **Inquiry**.
2. Tap the inquiry to see what they asked for, their notes and their inspiration photos.
3. Tap **Edit event**. The party is started from their party size and services.
4. Change the party, packages and timeline as you need.
5. Tap **Save changes**.

## Good to know

- The client is matched to their profile by phone or email, or added as a new client.
- They get a short message that the salon has their inquiry. Messages wait in the outbox until texts and email are connected.
- An owner can stop online inquiries on Settings, **Bridal and events** (**Take event inquiries on the booking site**).
