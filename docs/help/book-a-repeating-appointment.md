Book the same visit again and again, every few weeks, in one go.

1. Start a booking as usual: tap an empty time on the calendar (or **New booking**), choose the client and services.
2. Tick **Repeat this visit**.
3. Set **Every** how many weeks.
4. Choose how it ends: **For** a number of visits, or **Until** a date.
5. Tap **Check dates**. Each date shows **Fits** or **Doesn't fit**.
6. For a date that doesn't fit, pick one of Denise's suggestions or **Skip this date**.
7. Tap **Book** (it shows how many visits, for example **Book 6 visits**).

## Good to know

- Every visit uses the same stylist, services and time of day, unless you chose a suggestion.
- A series can have 2 to 52 visits.
- Booking details shows "Visit 2 of 6 · every 4 weeks" on each one.
- Editing, moving or canceling asks whether it's for this visit only, this and following visits, or all visits in the series.
