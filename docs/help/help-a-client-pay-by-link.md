What the client sees when they open a payment link, so you can walk them through it.

1. They tap the link. A page in the salon's name and colors opens with "Thanks for coming in" and their receipt.
2. Under **Add a tip**, there's a row for each stylist: they tap **18%**, **20%**, **25%** or **Custom**, or leave it with no tip.
3. They check the **Total** at the bottom.
4. They tap **Pay $…**.
5. They see **Paid. Thank you.**, and at the desk the checkout shows **Paid by link**.

## Good to know

- If the client's connection drops as they tap, their page says so and asks them to try again. They're never charged twice: if it went through, trying again says so.
- The link works once and only for 24 hours. An old one says "This link has expired"; a cancelled one says "This link is no longer active".
- Card payments online are in test mode for now: the page shows **Card (test mode: nothing is charged)** and no real money moves.
- The salon never sees or stores their card details.
- If the card is declined, the page says they haven't been charged and they can try another card, or pay at the desk.
