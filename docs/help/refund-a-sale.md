Give back some or all of what a client paid, take back returned products, and refund a gift card, package or membership the sale sold.

1. Go to **Sales** and tap the sale (see Find a past sale).
2. If products are coming back, enter how many under each "returned", and leave **Put returned products back in stock** ticked if they can be sold again.
3. If the sale sold a gift card, package or membership that's being refunded, tick **Refund** next to it. It shows how much comes back.
4. The **Amount ($)** fills in from what you ticked. Change it if needed; it says how much can be refunded.
5. In **Given back by**, choose **Card (on the terminal)**, **Cash**, or back onto the gift card it was paid with.
6. Type the **Reason** and tap **Refund**.

## Good to know

- Card refunds are done on the salon's own terminal; here you record them.
- You can refund more than once, up to what was paid. The sale shows **Part refunded** until it's all back.
- A gift card gives back only what's left on it, and the card is cancelled. A package gives back only the sessions not used, and it's cancelled. A membership ends today and gives back the period's price less the services it already covered.
- Money for a gift card, package or membership comes back only by ticking it.
- Returning a product takes back only that product's commission from the stylist. Their service commission and tips stay paid.
- Package uses, membership services and promotional credit can't be refunded as money.
- To undo the whole sale and reopen the visit instead, see Void a sale.
- The front desk can't refund; ask a manager or owner.

## If it doesn't work

- "That's more than was paid": lower the amount.
- "A gift card, package or membership is refunded by returning it…": tick it, and give back no more than it shows.
- "That was already cancelled or refunded": it's been refunded before; nothing more comes back for it.
