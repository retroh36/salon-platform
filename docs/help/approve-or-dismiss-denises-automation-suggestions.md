Denise looks at your salon's numbers and suggests automations worth turning on. You decide.

1. Go to **Settings** and tap **Automations**.
2. Read the box at the top, **Denise suggests**: each line says what to turn on and why (for example how many regulars are overdue).
3. Tap **Turn on** to turn that automation on as it's set up now.
4. Or tap **Not now**, and she won't suggest it again.
5. Changed your mind after turning one on? Tap **Undo** on that line to turn it back off.

## Good to know

- She only suggests automations that are ready to go as they are; check the message on its card first if you want it in your own words.
- **Undo** stays there for a day after you turn one on. After that, use **Turn off** on the automation's card.
- Denise never writes the messages: they're always your saved wording.
- No box at the top means she has nothing to suggest right now.
