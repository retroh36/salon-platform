See how many online bookings came from Google, Instagram, Facebook, your website and your automated messages, and what they brought in.

1. Go to **Reports**.
2. Pick the period at the top of the page: **This month**, **Last month** or **Last 30 days**.
3. Scroll to **Where online bookings came from**.
4. Read each row: **From** (where the client clicked), **Bookings**, **Came in** (checked out) and **Revenue**.

## Good to know

- Bookings are counted by the link the client followed. That's why each place needs its own link from **Settings** → **Online booking** (see Add your booking link to Google, Instagram and Facebook).
- **No tag (shared link, typed in)** means the plain link was used, or someone typed the address.
- Links in rebook reminders, win-back, birthday and review messages are counted under those names.
- If a client reschedules, the booking keeps where it first came from.
- Only owners can see Reports.
