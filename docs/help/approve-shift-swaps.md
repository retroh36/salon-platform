Approve or decline a shift swap two people on the front desk, or two assistants, have agreed between them.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Scheduling**, then the **Requests** tab.
2. Under **Shift swaps**, each one says who gives which day to whom, and their note.
3. Tap **Approve swap**. Their hours change straight away.
4. To say no, tap **Decline…**, write why (they'll both see it), then tap **Decline swap**.

## Good to know

- A swap shows here only once the coworker has accepted it.
- It can't be approved if it would put someone on two shifts at once or working through approved time off, or if their hours for those days have changed since it was asked.
- Both people get a notice in the app either way, and every step is logged.
