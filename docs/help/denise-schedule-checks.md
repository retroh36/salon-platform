Denise checks the team's week for gaps in cover, and drafts next month's schedule for you to check and publish.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Scheduling**. Denise's card above the week lists what she spotted: nobody on the front desk, no stylists working, no Master stylist that day, two people on one chair, draft hours over approved time off, or a time-off request on a busy day.
2. To plan next month, tap **Draft** and the month's name. Denise starts from everyone's usual hours, takes out approved time off, and covers front desk gaps with someone on the front desk who's off that day.
3. Read **Gaps I covered** and **Still to sort out**, then tap **Add to the draft**, or **Dismiss**.
4. Check each week in the grid, change anything you like, then tap **Publish week** as usual.

## Good to know

- Adding it to the draft changes nobody's hours. The team sees a week only once you publish it.
- **Undo** takes Denise's days back out of the draft. Weeks you've already published stay as they are.
- Denise never puts anyone on the front desk for more than 40 hours a week, and covers a gap with at least four hours.
- You can also ask Denise "Who's working Thursday night?" or "Can I give Nola Friday off?". She answers from the published schedule and changes nothing.
