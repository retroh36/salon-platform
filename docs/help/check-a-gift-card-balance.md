Look up a gift card by its code to see its balance and everything that's happened to it.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Gift cards** (under Sales).
2. Under **Check a balance**, scan or type the code into **Card code (enter or scan)**.
3. Tap **Check balance**.
4. The card opens: **Balance**, what it was sold for, when it **Expires**, and who it's **For**.
5. Scroll to **History** to see every sale, use, reload and change, with links to the sales.
6. To resend a card to its owner, use **Send it**: check the email address and tap **Email card**.

## Good to know

- You can also browse recent cards in the list below, with **Bought** and **Promotional** tabs.
- Each lookup is logged, and there's a limit of 30 lookups per person every 10 minutes.
- At checkout, the client's own cards and their balances show when you tap **Gift card** under Payment.
- Emailed cards wait in the outbox for now: email isn't connected yet.
