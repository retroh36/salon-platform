Set the sales tax added at checkout, who can give discounts and how much, and what receipts show.

1. Go to **Settings** and tap **Tax and receipts**.
2. Under **Sales tax**, enter the rates for **Services (%)** and **Retail products (%)**.
3. Under **Discounts**, tick **Managers can give discounts** and/or **The front desk can give discounts**.
4. Set **Most the front desk can take off (%)**.
5. Under **Receipts**, add **A note at the bottom (optional)** and tick **Show who did each service** if you want stylists' names on receipts.
6. Tap **Save**.

## Good to know

- Tax is added on top of prices; rates go from 0 to 25%. Gift cards are never taxed. Check what applies in your state and city.
- Owners can always give discounts, and every discount needs a reason.
- Member discounts from memberships don't count against the front desk's limit.
- Gift card and package settings have their own tabs in Settings.
