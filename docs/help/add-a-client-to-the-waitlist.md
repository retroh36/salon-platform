Put a client on the waitlist for when time opens up.

1. Go to **Calendar** and tap **Waitlist** at the top.
2. Under **Add to the waitlist**, find the client (or tap **New client**).
3. Choose the **Service**. Tap **Another service** for more (up to 5).
4. Tick the **Stylists** they'd see (none ticked means any stylist).
5. Set the dates: **From** and **Until** (up to three months apart).
6. Tick the **Times** that suit them: **Mornings**, **Afternoons**, **Evenings** (none ticked means any time).
7. Add **Notes (optional)**, then tap **Add to waitlist**.

## Good to know

- Everyone waiting is listed under **Waiting** with their services, stylists, dates and times. Tap **Remove** to take someone off.
- When a cancellation, no-show or Denise's fix frees time, she suggests who fits it in her panel (see Book a client from the waitlist).
- Stylists can see the waitlist but can't change it.
