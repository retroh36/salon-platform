List the salon's chairs, color bar, shampoo bowls and rooms, and give each stylist a home chair. Their clients are booked into it.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Settings**, then **Stations**.
2. Tap **Add station**.
3. Give it a **Name**, choose its **Type** and, if you like, **Where it is** (for example "Upstairs").
4. For a chair, choose whose home chair it is under **Home of**.
5. Tap **Add station**.
6. To change one, tap **Edit** on its row. Untick **In use** for a chair that's out of action.

## Good to know

- Each person has one home chair, and each chair one person. Choosing someone who already has a chair moves them.
- A barber's station can be someone's home too.
- Rooms are for services that need one: tick **Needs a room** on the service (Services, then the service).
- Home chairs show on the team schedule and on the calendar, next to each stylist's hours.
