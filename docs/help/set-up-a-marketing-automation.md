Turn on a message that goes out by itself, in your own words: **Rebook reminder**, **Win-back**, **Birthday** or **Review request**.

1. Go to **Settings** and tap **Automations**.
2. Find the automation's card and set its timing (for example **Days after they're due** or **Hours after checkout**).
3. Choose **Send by**: **Text, else email** or **Email, else text**.
4. Edit the **Email subject** and **Message**. Use only the fields listed under it, like {first_name}.
5. For review requests, add your **Google review link** or **Yelp page**. For birthdays, add an **Offer** if you like.
6. Check the **Preview**, tapping **Text** or **Email** to see each.
7. Tap **Turn on** (or **Save without turning on**). Once it's on, use **Save** for changes or **Turn off**.

## Good to know

- Only clients who said yes to texts or marketing emails get these, between 9am and 8pm, with an opt-out added to every message.
- Texts and email aren't connected yet, so messages wait in the outbox for now.
- Win-back counts the weeks from the day of their last visit at the salon, so an evening visit counts from that day.
- Each card shows results for 90 days: **Sent**, **Booked from it** (booked within 14 days), **Revenue** and **Opted out**.
- **Send due messages now** under **Sending** sends what's due without waiting for the hourly run.

## If it doesn't work

- **Turn on** is greyed out: the reasons are listed just above the buttons.
- A message with {booking_link} needs online booking turned on first.
