Cancel a payment link you sent, so you can change the ticket or take payment at the desk.

1. Open the visit's checkout (tap the visit, then **Check out**). It shows **Waiting for payment**.
2. Tap **Cancel the link**.
3. The ticket opens again: change it, take payment at the desk, or send a new link with **Text a payment link instead**.

## Good to know

- Once cancelled, the client's link says "This link is no longer active" and asks them to pay at the desk.
- If the link has expired, the checkout says **The payment link expired**; tap **Take payment here**.
- Completing the sale at the desk cancels any open link by itself.
- A link the client has already paid can't be cancelled. To give money back, see Refund a sale.
