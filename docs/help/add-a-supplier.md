Add a company you buy products from, so you can link products and orders to it.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Inventory**, then **Suppliers**.
2. Under **Add a supplier**, enter the **Name**.
3. Add the **Email**, **Phone** and **Account number** if you have them.
4. Enter **Days to deliver**, how long their deliveries usually take.
5. Tap **Add supplier**. You'll see "Added."

## Good to know

- Denise uses **Days to deliver** to decide when to suggest a reorder.
- Suppliers can't be edited or removed in the app yet.
- To link a product to a supplier, open the product, tap **Edit product** and choose the **Supplier**.
- Everyone can see the supplier list; only owners and managers can add to it.
