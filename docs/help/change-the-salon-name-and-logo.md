Your salon's name and logo show on the salon card at the top of the sidebar (under Maestra's logo) and on messages to your clients.

1. Go to **Settings** (the **Your salon** tab opens).
2. To rename, change **Salon name** and tap **Save name**.
3. To add a logo, tap the **Logo** file picker and choose an image.
4. Tap **Upload logo**.
5. To go back to no logo, tap **Remove logo**.

## Good to know

- Logos can be PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 2 MB. A square image works best.
- Without a logo, the salon's first initial shows instead.
- Tap the salon card to see your salon's locations, with yours ticked. Each team member works at one location for now.
- The name can be up to 100 characters.
- The Maestra logo on the sign-in screen stays the same; only your salon's own brand changes.
