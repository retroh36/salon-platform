See each automated message, whether it's on, and what it has done in the last 90 days.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Settings** (managers go straight to **Automations**).
2. Each card shows whether it's **On** or **Off**, when it goes out and the message.
3. Use **Preview** to see it as a **Text** or an **Email**, for a sample client.
4. Under the preview, read **Sent (90 days)**, **Booked from it**, **Revenue** and **Opted out**.

## Good to know

- Owners and managers turn automations on or off and edit the message. See "Set up a marketing automation".
- **Booked from it** counts clients who booked any way within 14 days of the message.
- Texts and email aren't connected yet, so messages wait in the outbox for now.
