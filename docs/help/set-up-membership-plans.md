Create the memberships staff can sell at checkout: what clients pay, how often, and what they get.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Settings**, then **Memberships**.
2. Under **New membership**, enter a **Name** and **Price ($)**, then how often under **Every** and **Billed by** (month or week).
3. Under **Included each period**, choose services and how many. Tick **Unused roll over** if leftovers carry over. Tap **Add a service** for more.
4. Enter any **Member discount on services (%)** and **Member discount on retail (%)**.
5. Under **Ending and pausing**, set the **Minimum term (periods)** and **Notice to end (days)**, and whether it **Can be paused** and **For up to (periods)**.
6. Tap **Add membership**.
7. To change one, tap **Edit** on it and tap **Save membership**. Untick **On sale** to stop selling it.

## Good to know

- Changes affect new members only: each member keeps the price and terms they joined on.
- Rolled-over services carry into the next period only.
- Payments are in test mode for now: no real card is charged.
