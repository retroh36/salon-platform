When the policy needs a card (or a deposit) for a booking made at the desk, send the client a secure link to add it.

1. On the calendar, tap the visit to open booking details.
2. Under the visit it says **Card needed to hold this booking** (or **Deposit needed**).
3. Tap **Send card link** (or **Send deposit link**).
4. Choose **Text** or **Email** and check the number or address.
5. Tap **Send link**.
6. The client opens it, reads the policy, ticks to agree and taps **Save card** (or **Pay … and save card**).

## Good to know

- Booking details says **link sent** until they've done it, then shows the card on file.
- Links work once, for 3 days.
- You can also send one from the client's profile, under **Cancellation policy**.
- Texts and email wait in the outbox for now, and cards are test cards.
