When the policy needs a card (or a deposit) for a booking made at the desk, send the client a secure link to add it. The booking form and Denise's booking card offer to text it as soon as you book; otherwise send it from booking details.

1. On the calendar, tap the visit to open booking details.
2. Under the visit it says **Card needed to hold this booking** (or **Deposit needed**).
3. Tap **Send card link** (or **Send deposit link**).
4. Choose **Text** or **Email** and check the number or address.
5. Tap **Send link**.
6. The client opens it, reads the policy, ticks to agree and taps **Save card** (or **Pay … and save card**).

## Good to know

- If the client's connection drops as they tap, their page says so and asks them to try again. They're never charged twice: if it went through, trying again says so.
- Booking details says **link sent** until they've done it, then shows the card on file.
- Links work once, for 3 days.
- A service's own deposit (set under Settings → Online booking) is asked for however the visit is booked: at the desk, by phone through Denise, from the waitlist, as a group or a rebook at checkout. Those last three say **Deposit needed** or **Card needed** in their confirmation, and booking details has the link.
- You can also send one from the client's profile: **Send card link** in the corner of the **Card on file** card. Once they have a card, that card shows it, when they agreed to the policy, and **Remove card**.
- Texts and email wait in the outbox for now, and cards are test cards.
