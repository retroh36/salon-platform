Put services that are usually booked together (Highlight, then Gloss, then Cut) into one bundle, with the time between them when the client processes. Staff book the bundle in one step, and each service still has its own stylist, price and checkout line.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Services**, then **Add bundle** at the top (or a bundle's name under **Bundles** to change it).
2. Give it a name, and a description if clients will book it online.
3. Choose the services in the order they're done. Tap **Add a service** for more, and use the arrows to put them in order. **×** removes one.
4. For each service after the first, set **Gap before**: the minutes between the service before ending and this one starting. The stylist is free for another client then.
5. Check the line under the list: the whole length, how long the stylist is free, and the services' menu prices added up.
6. Tick **Clients can book it online** if you want it on the booking site.
7. Tap **Add bundle** (or **Save bundle**).

## Good to know

- A bundle has 2 to 6 services, with gaps of up to 4 hours in 5-minute steps.
- A service built from steps keeps its own steps inside the bundle, so the stylist is free during its processing too.
- There's no bundle price: each service is charged at the stylist's price for it, with its own commission and tip at checkout. Payroll and reports see each service on its own.
- Changing a bundle changes only new bookings. Visits already booked keep their services and times.
- **Archive bundle** at the foot of its page takes it off the menu. **Restore bundle** puts it back.
