Give the client a receipt straight after the sale.

1. Tap **Complete sale** at checkout.
2. On the **Sale complete** screen, under **Receipt**, choose one:
3. **Print**: the receipt opens in a new tab with the print window.
4. **Email**: check the email address and tap **Send**.
5. **No receipt**: nothing is sent.
6. Tap **Back to calendar** (or **Back to sales**), or **Next client** to check out the next client who's here.

## Good to know

- Receipts list the lines by stylist, any discount and tips, and how it was paid.
- Email isn't connected yet: for now emailed receipts wait in the outbox and aren't actually delivered.
- To print or send it again later, see Reprint a receipt.
