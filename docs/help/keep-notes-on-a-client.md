Notes are what everyone should know about a client. They sit in a big box near the top of their profile.

1. Go to **Clients** and open the client's profile.
2. Tap the **Notes** box (on a keyboard, move to it and press Enter).
3. Type your notes. They save as you type; you'll see "Notes saved."
4. Tap **Done** when you're finished.

## Good to know

- Under the notes it says who changed them last and when, for example "Edited by Sam Rivera on Mon, Oct 26 at 2:14pm".
- Notes for one visit only belong in that visit's **Notes** tab in booking details instead.
- Stylists can read the notes but can't change them.
- Allergies have their own field (tap **Edit details**), so they show in red everywhere.

## If it doesn't work

- "Notes not saved: check the connection": the connection dropped. Keep typing, or tap outside the box, and they save again.
