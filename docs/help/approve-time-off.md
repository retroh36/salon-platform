Approve or decline time off your team has asked for.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Team**, then the **Schedule** tab.
2. At the top, find **Time off requests**, with the number waiting.
3. Check who it's for, the dates and the note.
4. Tap **Approve** or **Decline**.

## Good to know

- Approved time off blocks bookings for those times. Check the calendar for visits already booked then and move them.
- You can also approve or decline from the person's own **Time off** list.
- To take time off away later, tap **Remove** next to it in their **Time off** list.
