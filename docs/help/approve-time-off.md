Approve or decline time off your team has asked for, seeing who it affects first.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Scheduling**, then the **Requests** tab. The number waiting is on the tab.
2. Each request shows the dates, the note, **Clients booked** then, and who's **Also off then**. **Short notice** and **Blackout** tags say when it breaks the salon's rules.
3. With no clients booked, tap **Approve**.
4. With clients booked, tap **Approve and plan clients**. It approves it and opens the **Mark out** panel on the calendar, where Denise suggests where each client goes.
5. To say no, tap **Decline…**, write why (they'll see it), then tap **Decline request**.

## Good to know

- Approving doesn't move anyone. Under **Decided in the last 30 days**, an approved request with clients still booked has a **Plan …** link for each day.
- The person gets a notice in the app either way.
- Every request, decision and withdrawal is logged.
- Set the notice period and blackout dates on Settings → **Time off**. See "Set time-off rules".
