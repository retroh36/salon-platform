See what each person clocked against what they were scheduled, and fix a missed or wrong punch.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Scheduling**, then **Month**, then tap the person's name.
2. The totals show **Clocked so far** against their schedule. Each past day shows what they clocked, **No clock-out** or **Didn't clock in**.
3. Tap a day up to today to see its punches.
4. Write the **Reason for the change**, then change a time and tap **Save change**, tap **Remove**, or fill in **Add a punch** and tap **Add punch**.

## Good to know

- Every change is kept with who made it and why, in the Audit trail (Clocked hours).
- A manager can't change an owner's hours; an owner can.
- The Dashboard's **Time clock** card lists anyone not clocked in 10 minutes into their shift, or still clocked in 10 minutes after it ended.
- Times can't be in the future, punches can't overlap, and a punch is 18 hours at most.
