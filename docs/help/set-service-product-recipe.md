Set the back-bar products a service usually uses, so stock is taken off at checkout when no formula is recorded.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Services**.
2. Tap the service.
3. Scroll to **Product recipe**.
4. Tap **Add a product**, choose the product and enter the amount (in grams, ml or units).
5. Repeat for each product. Tap the × to remove one.
6. Tap **Save recipe**. You'll see "Recipe saved."

## Good to know

- Only back-bar products can be in a recipe.
- When the stylist records a formula with products for the visit, that's used instead of the recipe.
- Denise also uses recipes to work out what upcoming visits will need when she suggests reorders.
