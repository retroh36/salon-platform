See a whole month at a glance: how many visits each day has, how full it is and who's off.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Calendar**.
2. At the top right, tap **Month**.
3. Each day shows its visits, a bar for how much of the stylists' working time is booked, how many stylists are off, and any weddings or events.
4. Use the arrows to go to the month before or after, or tap **This month** to come back.
5. Tap a day to open it on the calendar. Tap **Day** to go back to the day you were on.

## Good to know

- Days the salon is closed say **Closed**.
- Stylists and assistants see their own book: their visits and how full their own day is.
- On a phone, the month is a list of days.
- Week view is still to come.
