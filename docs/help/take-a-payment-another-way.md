For money that isn't cash, a card or a gift card, such as a check or a bank transfer. You always say what it was, and owners and managers are told.

1. Open the checkout and check the **Total**.
2. Under **Payment**, tap **Other**.
3. Change the **Amount ($)** if only part of the sale is paid this way.
4. Under **What was it?**, type what it was, for example "Check #1043". **Record** stays grey until you do.
5. Tap **Record … other payment**, then **Complete sale**.

## Good to know

- Owners and managers (and anyone with **See the audit trail**) get an alert saying who took it, how much and your note. You don't get one for your own.
- It shows under **Paid by other** in the **Audit trail**, and on **Reconcile** in its own **Other** column with your note.
- It counts as money in, but not in **To the bank**: match it to your bank by its note.
- Voiding the sale takes it back as **Other** too. A refund goes back as cash, a card or a gift card.
