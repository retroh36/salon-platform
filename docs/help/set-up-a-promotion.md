A promotion takes a percent or an amount off what it covers, or sets its price: as a code, by itself at checkout, or as a last-minute special for open time.

1. In the sidebar, under **Sales**, tap **Promotions**, then **New promotion**.
2. Give it a **Name** and choose how it's used: **Promo code**, **Automatic** or **Last-minute special**.
3. For a code, type the **Code**. For a special, set **For open time starting within (hours)**.
4. Choose **Percent off**, **Amount off** or **Set price** and type the amount.
5. Under **What it covers**, tick **Services**, **Retail** or both, and narrow it to categories, services, stylists, levels or products if you like.
6. To leave something out, tap **Add a "never"** and pick a level, a category or both (for example Master and Color: never Master stylists' color).
7. To keep it to certain days or hours, tick **Only on these days** and fill in **From** and **Until**. Set dates, uses in all, uses per client and a minimum spend, and tick **New clients only**, **A client's first visit only** or **Can be used with other promotions and discounts** as needed.
8. Check the line that sums it up, then tap **Create promotion**.

## Good to know

- Specials show a badge on the times they cover on your booking site. The client sees "Last-minute 15% off" and the price after it on review, on their confirmation and in their account, and it comes off at checkout.
- A set price is per service or product it covers, and never raises something already cheaper. With **Only these levels**, one promotion can be "$30 blowout, Artisan only, Tuesdays 12 to 3".
- Days and hours count from when the visit starts, or the moment of the sale when there's no visit.
- An **Automatic** promotion with days or hours shows its price or discount on the matching times on your booking site, only for the stylists it covers. Untick **Show on matching times on the booking site** to keep it to checkout.
- A promotion can't be used with another discount unless you tick **Can be used with other promotions and discounts**.
- Member prices come off first, then promotions, then any discount staff give.
- Owners and managers see under each promotion how it did in the last 90 days: sales, what they brought in, what the discount cost, openings filled and first visits. Reports has the same for any period.
- **Edit** changes it from now on; **End** stops it being used, and it stays in Reports.

## If it doesn't work

- "Another promotion already uses that code": choose another code, or end the old one first.
