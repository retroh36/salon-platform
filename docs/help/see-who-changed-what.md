The audit trail lists every change with who made it. That includes every move, cancel, no-show and check-in on a visit, and every sale, void, refund and discount. It also lists gift card and fee changes, points adjustments, pay rate changes, changes to clocked hours, PTO adjustments and going live.

1. In the sidebar, under **Sales**, tap **Audit trail**.
2. Under **What**, choose a type, or leave it on **Everything**.
3. Under **Who**, choose a person, or leave it on **Everyone**.
4. Choose the dates under **From** and **To**, and tap **Show**.
5. Tap a line to open the visit, sale, gift card or client it's about.

## Good to know

- Owners and managers see it. An owner can let someone on the front desk see it too: open their page under **Team** and turn on **See the audit trail**.
- Nothing in it can be edited or deleted. The database writes every visit change itself, whether it was made on the calendar, online by the client, or by one of Denise's fixes.
- A visit's own changes are also in booking details, under **History**.
- **Discounts and $0 sales** lists completed sales with a staff discount or a total of $0.
- **Team purchases** lists every sale to someone on the team at the employee price, with who it was for and who rang it up.
- **Paid by other** lists every payment taken as **Other**, with the note staff gave.
- **Cash drawer** lists opening counts a manager skipped, with the reason, and counts that were over or short.
- It shows up to 92 days at a time, and the latest 300 lines.

## If it doesn't work

- **Audit trail** isn't in the sidebar: you don't have the audit permission. Ask an owner.
