Plan everyone's shifts for a week on one grid, then publish it. Until you publish, your changes are a draft nobody else sees.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Scheduling**.
2. Use the arrows or **This week** to choose the week.
3. Tap a shift, or an empty day in someone's row. A small box opens beside it with the start and end times. For a split shift, tap **+ Add split shift**. For a day off, tap **Day off**.
4. Tap **Save**. Draft shifts have a dashed outline. **Cancel**, Escape or a tap outside closes the box without saving.
5. To move a shift to another day, drag it onto that day in the same row.
6. **Copy last week** copies last week's hours into this week; **Clear week** takes every shift off.
7. When the week is ready, tap **Publish week**, check the changes, then tap **Publish and tell**.

## Good to know

- Each day shows hours scheduled (less approved time off) and hours booked with clients.
- Each shift shows both its times, like 9–5. A split shift is two, like 9–1 and 2–6. Days the salon is closed say **Closed** at the top.
- A warning sign on a day means clients are booked outside the new hours. Publishing never moves anyone, so you choose: **Publish and move clients** opens the Mark out panel on the first day, or **Publish, move them later** keeps **Move** links on the page.
- Once you publish, the new hours reach everyone at once, with no reload: every open calendar (the front desk iPad too), online booking, Denise and each stylist's My day.
- Everyone whose hours change gets a notice on My day.
- **Discard changes** throws the week's draft away.
- Past days can't be changed. The front desk sees the published week, read-only.
