Plan everyone's shifts for a week on one grid, then publish it. Until you publish, your changes are a draft nobody else sees.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Scheduling**.
2. Use the arrows or **This week** to choose the week.
3. Tap a day in someone's row to add or change their hours. For a split shift, tap **+ Add a split shift**. For a day off, tap **Day off**.
4. Tap **Save to draft**. Draft shifts have a dashed outline.
5. To move a shift, drag it, onto another day too. Drag either end to change its times.
6. **Copy last week** copies last week's hours into this week; **Clear week** takes every shift off.
7. When the week is ready, tap **Publish week**, check the changes, then tap **Publish and tell**.

## Good to know

- Each day shows hours scheduled (less approved time off) and hours booked with clients.
- A warning sign on a day means clients are booked outside the new hours. Publishing doesn't move them: use the **Move** links after publishing, which open the Mark out panel.
- Everyone whose hours change gets a notice on My day.
- **Discard changes** throws the week's draft away.
- Past days can't be changed. The front desk sees the published week, read-only.
