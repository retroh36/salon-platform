When a stylist is out (all day or from a time), see where every one of their clients can go, change any line, and move them all at once.

1. Go to **Calendar**, tap **⋯** (More for the stylist's name) at the top of their column, then **Plan the rescue**. Or open the stylist on **Team** and tap **Plan the rescue**, or tell Denise ("Nola called out", "Gil has to leave at 1") and tap **Open sick-day rescue**.
2. Choose **All day** or **From a time** (and when they leave), then tap **Mark … out**. This adds approved time off, so nothing else is booked with them.
3. Read the plan. Each client starts on Denise's best choice: the same time with a stylist at the same level, then another stylist or a little later that day, then another day. Clients who only see this stylist are offered their next openings with them.
4. To change a client, choose another option in **Where they go**, or **Leave it and call the client**. A line marked **Clashes with a line above** needs another choice.
5. Tap **Apply all**. Every client moves at once, or none do if something changed meanwhile.
6. Under **Messages to clients**, check each message, change the words if you need to, and tap **Send** (or **Send all**).

## Good to know

- Each option is checked with the booking rules: who does the service, each stylist's own length for it (where Denise knows it), processing time and chairs. A stylist at another level charges their own price, shown on the option ("Master · $20 more").
- Clients are planned in the order of their visits; two who start at the same time go in the order they booked, so whoever booked first gets the first pick.
- The stylists taking on a client get a notice under **Changes to your day** on My day.
- Checkout credits the stylist who does the service, so commission and tips go to them.
- **Undo all** puts everyone back where they were, as long as nothing has changed since. Messages already sent aren't taken back, so message those clients again.
- Messages go only the way each client allows for visit messages; if they don't get any, the message shows **No messages: call**. Your role may only be able to send the template as it is (Settings → Messages).
- Clients set to **Only books with their preferred stylist** (on their details) are never moved to someone else.
- Stylists can mark themselves out from their own column; the front desk plans where their clients go.
