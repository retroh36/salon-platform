A permission group is a set of switches you give to several people at once, like "Senior front desk". Change the group and everyone in it changes.

1. In the sidebar, under **Salon**, tap **Team**, then **Permissions**.
2. Tap **New group** and give it a name.
3. For each switch, choose **On**, **Off** or **Role's default**.
4. Tap **Add group**.
5. To put someone in it, go to **Directory**, tap their name, and choose it under **Permission group**.

## Good to know

- **Edit** changes the group for everyone in it. **Copy** makes a new group with the same switches and nobody in it yet. **Remove** asks first, then puts its people back on their role's defaults.
- **Role's default** leaves that switch to each person's role.
- A group never gives a role more than it can have. For example, a group with **Salon settings** on gives it to managers but not to the front desk.
- A switch changed for one person on their page wins over their group.
- Owners can always do everything, so they aren't in groups.
- Denise follows the same switches when she answers.
- Changes apply the next time each person opens a page.
