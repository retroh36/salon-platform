Add or take off a client's rewards points by hand, with a reason, and see every change to them.

1. Go to **Clients** and tap the client's name.
2. Find the **Rewards** card on the right of their profile: their points, tier and a bar to the next tier.
3. Tap **Adjust points** in the card's top right corner.
4. Under **Points**, enter the points to add (like 50) or take off (like -50).
5. Under **Why**, say why. Tap **Save**. The card shows the new balance.
6. To see every change, tap **View history**.

## Good to know

- Every change is kept with who made it and why.
- An owner can let the front desk adjust points too: Team, their page, **Adjust rewards points**.
- The client's referral code is in **Rewards** too, with **Copy**. They can copy their referral link from their online account.

## If it doesn't work

- No **Adjust points** button: you need the **Adjust rewards points** permission.
- No **Rewards** on the profile: the rewards program is off (Settings, Rewards).
