Each person has one role: Owner, Manager, Front desk, Stylist, Assistant or Bookkeeper. The sidebar hides what you can't use, and an owner can switch extra things on or off for one person.

1. Tap your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open **Your account**: **What you can do** lists anything beyond your role's basics.
2. **Bookkeepers**: the money and nothing of the calendar or clients: **Reports** (with **Reconcile**), **Sales** (every day, read only, without client names), **Policy** and the **Audit trail**. An owner can add **Payroll** (which also changes pay rates) and **Download the salon's data**. They can't sell, refund, void or close out the day, and Denise isn't on their pages.
3. **Assistants**: **My day** shows the day of the stylist they assist (no prices, no timing taps), with that stylist's clients and their own pay. They can't book or check anyone in.
4. **Stylists**: **My day** (their visits, service timer, earnings, tips and goals), the calendar and the clients on their own book, notes and formulas on their visits, Team, Services, Inventory (view) and Forms. No salon money.
5. **Front desk**: the calendar, booking, check-in and checkout, every client, **Sales** (today's), **Gift cards**, selling retail and receiving orders. Discounts up to the salon's limit. No payroll, pay rates or reports.
6. **Managers**: everything but billing (the plan and Denise's limit) and deleting the business: **Reports**, **Payroll**, **Policy**, **Settings**, voids and refunds, and adding, inviting and deactivating anyone but owners.
7. **Owners**: everything, including billing, making someone an owner, sending a password reset and changing what one person can do.

## Good to know

- A page you can't use sends you back to the Dashboard (a bookkeeper, to Reports).
- Denise's answers follow the same rules: stylists and assistants only hear about their own book, and sales only go to those with reports.
- Roles are set on the Team page by owners and managers; what one person can do is set by an owner on their Team page.
