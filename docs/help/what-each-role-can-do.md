Each person has one role: Owner, Manager, Front desk or Stylist. The sidebar hides what your role can't use.

1. Check your role at the bottom of the sidebar, under your name.
2. **Stylists**: **My day** (their visits, service timer, their earnings), the calendar (view, block their own time), clients (view, notes and formulas on their visits), Team, Services, Inventory (view) and Forms. No salon money.
3. **Front desk**: everything a stylist sees except **My day**, plus booking and changing visits, check-in and checkout, **Sales**, **Gift cards**, editing clients, selling retail and receiving orders. Discounts only if the owner allows, up to the set limit. No payroll or money reports.
4. **Managers**: everything the front desk does, plus **Payroll**, **Durations**, voids and refunds, inventory, orders and stocktakes, services, schedules and staff rates, and adding, inviting and deactivating anyone but owners. Under **Settings** they can see **Automations** (each message and what it's done) but not change them.
5. **Owners**: everything, plus **Reports** and **Settings** (online booking, tax and receipts, gift cards, packages, memberships, automations, payroll periods, your data). Only owners change automations, make someone an owner, or send someone a password reset.

## Good to know

- A page you can't use sends you back to the Dashboard.
- Denise's answers follow the same rules: stylists only hear about their own figures, and only owners and managers hear about sales.
- Roles are set on the Team page by owners and managers; ask them if yours looks wrong.
