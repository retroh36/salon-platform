Start checkout for a visit from the calendar.

1. On the calendar, tap the client's visit to open booking details.
2. Make sure they're checked in (if not, tap **Check in** first).
3. Tap **Check out** at the bottom.
4. The checkout screen opens with the visit's services on the ticket. Add products, tips and payment there, then complete the sale.

## Good to know

- Nothing is lost if you leave: the ticket is saved as you build it, and **Check out** opens it again.
- When the sale is done the visit shows a check mark on the calendar, in its service color a little paler ("Checked out").
- To reverse a checkout, a manager or owner voids the sale. Booking details shows a link to it.
- For the checkout screen itself (payments, tips, rebooking), see the checkout help pages.
- **Check out** on a client's profile opens today's visit, or a new sale for them if they have none today.

## If it doesn't work

- "We couldn't reach the server…" after **Complete sale**: check the Wi-Fi, then tap **Complete sale** again. If the sale had already gone through, it shows as done; it's never recorded twice.
- "The ticket isn't saved: check the connection…" when you go back: the checkout stays open so nothing is lost. Once the Wi-Fi is back, go back again.
