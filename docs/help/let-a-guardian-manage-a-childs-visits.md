A dependent is someone in a family, usually a child, whose visits another family member looks after.

1. Open the child's profile. If they aren't in a family yet, tap **Add family** on the **Family** card and add the parent.
2. On the **Family** card, choose the parent under **Dependent of**.

## Good to know

- The guardian sees the dependent's visits in their online account, and can change or cancel them there, or tap **Book for** and the dependent's name.
- Booking confirmations, changes, cancellations and reminders for the dependent go to the guardian.
- Adults in a family never see each other's visits.
- Someone who leaves the family stops being a dependent.
- Messages staff write themselves still go to the contact on the dependent's own profile.
