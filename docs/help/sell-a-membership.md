Sign a client up for a membership at checkout. Today's payment covers the first period, and it renews by itself after that.

1. Open the checkout: tap **Check out** in booking details, or **New sale** on **Sales**.
2. For a new sale, find the client with **Find a client** at the top.
3. Under the lines, tap **Membership**.
4. Choose the plan. Each shows its price and what's included.
5. Under **Card it renews on**, choose the card.
6. Tap **Add to ticket**.
7. Take today's payment under **Payment**, then tap **Complete sale**.

## Good to know

- Payments are in test mode for now: you pick a test card and no real card is saved or charged. Once Stripe is connected, the client's card is saved on the reader.
- The **Membership** button only shows when an owner has set up plans on Settings, Memberships.
- A client can't join the same plan twice; plans they already have are greyed out.
- Member benefits start on this sale: see Use membership benefits at checkout.
- If the sale is voided before the membership is used or renewed, it's canceled.

## If it doesn't work

- "Memberships need a client on the sale": add the client first.
