Name the kinds of time off the team can ask for, and set how paid time off (PTO) is earned.

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Time off**.
2. Under **Types of time off**, change a **Name**, choose what it **Shows as** on the calendar, and tick **Uses PTO** for the types that come out of the balance. Tap **Add a type** for another, or **Remove**. Then tap **Save types**.
3. Under **PTO**, tick **Track PTO balances**.
4. Choose **How it's earned**: **A yearly amount** (hours at the start of each PTO year), **Per hour worked** (say, 1 hour for every 30 worked) or **Per pay period**.
5. Choose when **The PTO year starts**, the day **Balances start from**, what happens **At the year's end** (carry over up to a limit, everything or nothing), and **Most anyone can hold** (leave it blank for no cap).
6. Tick **Who earns PTO**, then tap **Save PTO policy**.

## Good to know

- Time off uses the person's own working hours on each day: a full day off uses that day's shift, part of a day only what it covers.
- Per hour worked counts clocked hours if the team uses the time clock, else scheduled hours.
- Earning stops at the cap. At the end of the PTO year, anything over the carry-over limit is lost.
- Someone who starts partway through a year gets part of the yearly amount, unless you untick that.
- A removed type stays on time off already taken under it.
- Changing the policy recalculates every balance from the day balances start. To give someone hours from before, adjust their balance on Scheduling → **Balances**.
