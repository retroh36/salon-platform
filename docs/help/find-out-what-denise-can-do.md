Meet Denise is one page on everything Denise does for the salon, with examples from her cards, and the rules she works by.

1. Open Denise (her face in the corner, or **Ask Denise** in the sidebar) and tap **What can Denise do?** in her header, or go to **Settings** → **Denise**.
2. Read down the page: fixing the day live, the morning brief, what she learns, ordering, booking by typing, answering questions and growing the book.
3. Under **Trust and guardrails**, see what she never does on her own.
4. Under **Try it**, tap a question. Denise opens with it typed in her box.
5. Tap **Send** to ask it.

## Good to know

- Everyone on the team can open the page; the sample questions match what your role can ask.
- Nothing on the page asks Denise anything until you send a question yourself.
- What's marked **Coming soon** (texts and emails reaching clients, reminders before visits, live card payments) isn't live yet.
