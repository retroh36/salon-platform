Messages about visits (confirmations, reminders, changes) are separate from offers. Turn them on or off for texts and email.

1. Go to **Clients** and open the client's profile.
2. Scroll to **Messages**.
3. Under **About their visits**, tick or untick **Texts** and **Emails**. It saves by itself.
4. Tap **Consent history** to see every change, with when, how and who.

## Good to know

- A client who texts STOP gets no more texts at all, and shows **No texts — call or email** on their profile and in booking details. Messages about visits go by email instead.
- Only the client can undo a STOP, by texting START. Staff can't turn texts back on for them.
- Clients choose these themselves when booking online and in their online account.
- Turning off offers never stops messages about visits.

## If it doesn't work

- **Texts** can't be ticked: the client replied STOP, or there's no phone number.
