Book a visit for a client from the calendar, with one or more services.

1. Go to **Calendar** and pick the day (use the arrows, **Today**, or the date picker).
2. Tap an empty time in the stylist's column, or tap **New booking** at the top. (Or tap **Book** on the client's profile: the booking opens with them already chosen, with their stylist.)
3. Under **Find a client**, search by name or phone and tap the client. New? Tap **New client**, enter their name and **Phone**, then tap **Add client**.
4. Under **Service 1**, choose the **Service**, then check the **Stylist**, **Starts** time and **Chair** (**Any free chair** is fine). Tick **Client asked for …** when the client asked for that stylist.
5. To add more, tap **Add another service**. It starts straight after the one before.
6. Add any **Notes**, check the total time and price at the bottom, then tap **Create booking**.

## Good to know

- **New booking** today starts at the next quarter hour. A time that has already passed shows a note but can still be booked, for recording a visit after it happened.
- **Client asked for …** starts ticked when the stylist is the client's preferred one. Denise's fixes never move a service the client asked for to another stylist. Booking details shows **Asked for** by the stylist's name. Online bookings where the client chose a stylist are marked the same way.
- If the stylist usually takes longer, Denise offers a button (like **Book 1 hr 45 min**) to book their usual length.
- If the cancellation policy needs a card or a deposit for this booking, it says **Card needed to hold this booking** or **Deposit needed** above the total, with **Text … a card link after booking** (or a deposit link) ticked. Untick it to send the link later from booking details.
- A patch test warning shows if the service needs one. If the salon requires it, you can't book until one is recorded (see Record a patch test).
- Services usually booked together are under **Bundles** at the top of the **Service** list. See "Book a service bundle".
- For several people, tap **Book a group instead**. To keep a slot free, tap **Block this time instead**.
- Closing a booking you've started (or tapping elsewhere) asks first: **Keep editing** or **Discard changes**.
- A stylist is never booked with two clients at once, and a chair never holds two: Maestra itself refuses it, even when two people book the same time on two screens. A stylist can still take a client while another's color processes.
- On the phone? Type what the client asks for in Denise's chat to get the best times (see Book by asking Denise).

## If it doesn't work

- The time is refused: the reason shows on the service, and Denise offers the nearest times that work. Tap one to use it.
- Tapping the calendar only closes the side panel: tap the empty time again.
