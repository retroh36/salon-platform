**Events** shows every event as a card in its column: **Inquiry**, **Proposal sent**, **Booked**, **Trial done**, **Event day** and **Completed**.

1. Go to **Events**.
2. Tap a card to open the event.
3. If the client won't go ahead, tap **Mark as lost**, say why, and tap **Mark as lost** again.
4. To bring a lost event back, open it and tap **Reopen**. It goes back to **Inquiry**.

## Good to know

- Events move by themselves: sending the proposal moves the event to **Proposal sent**, and the client signing and paying the deposit moves it to **Booked**.
- On the day itself, a booked event shows under **Event day**.
- Lost events are listed under the board with the reason. Marking an event lost cancels its open links and the payments still to come. Paid payments stay paid.
