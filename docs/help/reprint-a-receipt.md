Print or email a receipt again for a past sale.

1. Go to **Sales**.
2. Use **← Previous day** to find the day of the sale.
3. Tap the sale in the list.
4. Under **Receipt** on the right, tap **Print receipt**, or check the email address and tap **Email receipt**.

## Good to know

- Voided sales have no receipt to print or send.
- Every emailed receipt is noted in the sale's **History**.
- Email isn't connected yet: for now emailed receipts wait in the outbox and aren't actually delivered.
