On your phone, tap when each service starts, goes into processing, comes back to the chair and finishes. It's optional.

1. Go to **My day**. Your clients for today are listed in order.
2. When the client sits down, tap **Start service**. This also checks them in.
3. If the service has processing time, tap **Processing** when the color goes on.
4. When they come back, tap **Back in chair**.
5. When you're done, tap **Finish service**.
6. Tapped the wrong one? Tap **Undo last tap**.

## Good to know

- Tapping is optional. When you do, Denise learns how long you really take; otherwise she goes by check-in and checkout.
- What Denise learns helps book your usual length for each service and spot visits that might run over.
- The front desk still checks clients out.
- Your pay for the period is further down the page (see See my earnings).
