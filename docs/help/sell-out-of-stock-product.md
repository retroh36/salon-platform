Sell a product the system shows as out of stock, for example when there's one on the shelf the count missed.

1. Add the product to the ticket as usual. The line shows "Only … in stock".
2. Tap the line to open it.
3. Front desk: tap **Manager OK**. A manager or owner types their **Manager's email** and **Password** and taps **OK the sale**.
4. Managers and owners: tap **Sell anyway**, then **Sell anyway** again to confirm.
5. Take payment and tap **Complete sale**.

## Good to know

- The OK is for this product on this checkout only, and it's recorded as that manager's OK.
- The manager's password is only checked, never saved.
- If stock is often wrong, ask a manager to do a stocktake in Inventory.

## If it doesn't work

- **Complete sale** is greyed out with "… is out of stock: a manager needs to OK selling it.": get the manager's OK first.
- "Only a manager or owner can OK that.": the login used isn't a manager's or owner's.
