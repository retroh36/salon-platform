Add a client who walked in to the queue, see who's free next, and book them.

1. Go to **Calendar**, tap **More** at the top, then **Walk-ins**.
2. Under **Add a walk-in**, enter their **Name**, **Phone (optional)** and choose the **Service**.
3. Tap **Add to queue**.
4. Each person in the queue shows **Next free:** the stylist who does that service, the time and the wait.
5. Tap **Book with** (stylist) **at** (time) to book them.
6. If they leave without waiting, tap **Left**. Tap **Done** to close the panel.

## Good to know

- Walk-ins always go on today's calendar, even if you're looking at another day.
- If no one who does the service is free today, it says so.
- Once booked (with today's calendar showing), the visit opens in booking details so you can check them in.
