Cards on file, deposits, no-show and late-cancel fees, payment links and membership renewals go through your own Stripe account, so the money goes straight to the salon. Until you go live, only test cards work and no money moves.

1. Go to **Settings** and tap **Payments**.
2. Tap **Connect with Stripe**. Stripe opens: sign in to your Stripe account, or make one, and fill in your business and bank details.
3. When Stripe sends you back, **Stripe account** says **Ready**. If it says **Needs details**, tap **Finish setting up Stripe**.
4. Try it with Stripe's test card while **Payments** says **Test mode**.
5. Tap **Go live**. **Payments** says **Live**, and clients' cards are charged for real.
6. To stop taking real payments, tap **Stop taking real payments**.

## Good to know

- Only owners connect Stripe and go live. Managers can see the status.
- Card numbers go straight to Stripe. Maestra never sees or stores them.
- Card payments at the desk are still taken on your own card machine and recorded at checkout.
- **Disconnect Stripe…** puts payments back to test cards only. Cards clients saved stay in your Stripe account.
- The demo and test copies of Maestra never charge real cards.

## If it doesn't work

- **Connect with Stripe** or **Go live** is greyed out: the reason is just under it, for example Stripe isn't set up on Maestra yet.
- A client's card was declined: the reason shows where they entered it, and on the fee or payment that didn't go through.
