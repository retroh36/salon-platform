Add money to a gift card someone already has.

1. Go to **Sales** and tap **New sale** (or add it to a visit's checkout).
2. Under the lines, tap **Gift card**.
3. Tap **Reload a card**.
4. Tap a preset amount, or type it in **Amount ($)**.
5. Scan or type the card's code into **Card code (type or scan)**.
6. Tap **Add to ticket**.
7. Take payment with **Card** or **Cash**, then tap **Complete sale**.

## Good to know

- A reload is $5 to $1,000.
- Only cards that were bought can be reloaded. Promotional cards can't.
- A reload can't be paid for with a gift card.

## If it doesn't work

- "Promotional cards can't be reloaded": sell a new gift card instead (see Sell a gift card).
- **Complete sale** is greyed out: the reason is shown just above it.
