See the hours you're working this week, as your manager published them.

1. In the sidebar, tap **My day** (stylists and assistants), or **Team**, then **Schedule** (everyone).
2. **My week** on My day lists today and the six days after it, with any approved time off.
3. On **Schedule**, use **← Previous** and **Next →** to see other weeks.

## Good to know

- When a manager publishes a change to your hours, a notice appears at the top of My day and your Schedule page. Tap to dismiss it.
- Draft changes a manager hasn't published yet don't show here.
- The front desk can see the whole team's published week: on **Schedule**, tap **Team schedule**.
