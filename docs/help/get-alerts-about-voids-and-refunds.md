Someone may void or refund a sale, complete a $0 sale, adjust or void a gift card, or waive a cancellation fee. When they do, owners and managers get an alert saying who did it, what and why. The person who did it doesn't get one.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Dashboard**.
2. Read your alerts under **For you**.
3. Tap **Got it** when you've read them.
4. To see everything, tap **Audit trail**.

## Good to know

- Alerts go to everyone who can see the audit trail: owners, managers, and anyone on the front desk an owner has given **See the audit trail**.
- Time-off and shift swap requests waiting for you are under **For you** too.
- Nobody else can read your alerts.
- Alerts are in the app for now. They'll come by email too once email is set up.
