Set the stock level that counts as low, and the fewest containers to order.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Inventory**.
2. Tap the product.
3. Under **Adjust**, find **Reorder below** and enter the amount.
4. In **Order at least**, enter the fewest containers to order (a case of 6, say).
5. Tap **Save**. You'll see "Saved."

## Good to know

- A product below its reorder point shows **Low stock** on Inventory, and on the Dashboard when today's visits need it.
- Tap the low stock alert on Inventory to see only those products.
- Denise uses these numbers, and upcoming bookings, when drafting reorders: never fewer than **Order at least**, and more when bookings need it. See "Review Denise's reorder drafts".
