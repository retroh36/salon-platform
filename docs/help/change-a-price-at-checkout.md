Charge a different price for a service on the ticket, with a reason.

1. Open the checkout for the visit (tap the visit, then **Check out**).
2. Tap the service line on the ticket. It opens to edit.
3. Type the new amount in **Price ($)**.
4. In **Why the price changed (was $…)**, type the reason (for example "extra color for long hair").
5. Tap the line again (or press Escape) to close it.

## Good to know

- The receipt shows the booked price and your reason under the line.
- To take money off instead of changing the price, use a discount: see Give a discount at checkout.
- You can also change who did the service in **Done by**.
- Services paid with a package or membership can't have their price changed.

## If it doesn't work

- **Complete sale** is greyed out with "Say why … price changed.": fill in the reason.
