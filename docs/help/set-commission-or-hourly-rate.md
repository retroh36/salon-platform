Set the service and retail commission and the hourly rate for a team member.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Team**, then the **Schedule** tab.
2. Tap the person's name in the row of names at the top.
3. Scroll to **Level and commission**.
4. Enter **Service commission (%)** and **Retail commission (%)**.
5. For anyone paid by the hour, enter **Hourly rate ($)**. Leave it empty if they aren't hourly.
6. Tap **Save**. You'll see "Saved."

## Good to know

- A new rate only counts from now on. Pay for past sales never changes, because each sale keeps the rate in effect when it was made.
- Hourly pay uses the rate in effect on each day, so a raise mid-period is split correctly.
- Payroll shows the rates used, for example "45%, 50%" after a change.
- Team members can see their own rates on the same page, but can't change them.
- For tiers, a back-bar fee or booth rent, put the person on a plan on Settings → Commission. Their own rates then apply only if they come off the plan; the hourly rate always applies.
