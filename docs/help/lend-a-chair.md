On a day a stylist is off, let someone else use their chair for their shift ("Shae is using Chair 4 today").

1. In the sidebar, tap **Scheduling**, then **Floor today**.
2. Use the arrows to choose the day.
3. Find the chair. If its stylist is off, tap **Lend Chair 4…** under it.
4. Choose **Who's using it**.
5. Tap **Lend it**.

## Good to know

- It's lent for that person's working hours that day. Their clients are booked into it.
- A chair can't be lent while its own stylist is working, and two people can't have the same station at once.
- To end it early, tap **Give back** next to the name.
- The lent chair shows on the team schedule that day and on the calendar next to the person's hours.
- Floor today also shows clients booked into rooms. The front desk can see it but not change it.
