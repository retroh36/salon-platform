Clock in when your shift starts and out when it ends, on your phone or at the front desk.

1. On your phone: in the sidebar, tap **My day**, then **Clock in** under Time clock. Tap **Clock out** when you leave.
2. At the front desk iPad: on the PIN screen, tap **Clock in or out**, tap your name, enter your PIN and tap **Clock in or out**. It says whether you're now in or out. Nobody is signed in.
3. For a break, clock out and clock in again.

## Good to know

- Forget, and you get a reminder in the app 10 minutes into your shift, or 10 minutes after it ends while you're still clocked in.
- Managers see who hasn't clocked in on the Dashboard, and fix a missed or wrong punch on your month in Scheduling.
- Clocked in for more than 18 hours? Ask a manager to fix it: your next clock-in starts afresh.
- Only salons that use the time clock show it (Settings → Shared devices, "The team clocks in and out").
