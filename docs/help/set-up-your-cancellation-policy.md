Set when clients need a card on file, how much notice they must give, and what a late cancellation or no-show costs.

1. Go to **Settings** and tap **Policies**.
2. Under **Ask for a card to book**, choose **For every booking**, **For new clients** or **For some services or stylists**.
3. Type the **Cancellation window (hours before the visit)**.
4. Choose the **Late cancellation** and **No-show** fees: a fixed amount, a percent of the visit, or the deposit.
5. To charge late cancels only on visits that held a spot for weeks, fill in **Only charge the late-cancel fee for visits booked at least this many days ahead**.
6. Under **Repeat offenders**, set how many late cancels or no-shows in how many months, and what they then pay ahead.
7. Tick **Who can waive a fee**, check **What clients see**, and tap **Save policy**.
8. For a stricter stylist, tap **Edit** next to them under **Stricter rules for a stylist**.

## Good to know

- Clients read the policy in plain language and agree before their card is saved; what they agreed to is kept on their profile.
- Fees are charged to the card on file 10 minutes after a no-show or late cancellation, so a mistake can be undone first.
- Cancellations by the salon never cost the client, and their deposit is refunded.
- Cards are test cards for now: no real money moves until card payments are connected.
