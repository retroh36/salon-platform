See everyone at your location, with their role, level and contact details.

1. In the sidebar, under **Salon**, tap **Team**.
2. Stay on the **Directory** tab.
3. Find the person. Each row shows their name, role, level, email and phone.
4. Tap an email address to write to them, or a phone number to call.
5. For hours, time off and pay rates, tap the **Schedule** tab.

## Good to know

- Adding a new team member, or changing someone's contact details, isn't in the app yet.
- Someone who has left shows as **Inactive**.
- Stylist photos for online booking are uploaded on the Schedule tab, under the stylist's name (owners and managers).
