Text or email a client a secure link to fill in and sign the forms their visit needs on their own phone, before they come in. Handy for visits booked by phone.

1. On the calendar, tap the visit to open booking details.
2. Tap **Before this visit** to open the checklist.
3. Under the forms, tap **Send form link**.
4. Choose **Text** or **Email** and check the number or address.
5. Tap **Send link**.
6. The client opens the link, answers each form and taps **Sign and submit**. Each form they sign is ticked off under **Before this visit**.

## Good to know

- When you book a new visit that needs forms (a first-visit consultation, or consent for some services), the booking form and Denise's booking card show **Form to sign** with **Text … the form to sign before the visit** ticked. The link goes as soon as it's booked. Untick it to skip.
- One link holds every form the visit still needs. Sending again replaces the earlier link.
- The link works until the visit starts, for 2 weeks at most. A form signed at the desk in the meantime counts on the link too.
- Signed forms are kept on the client's profile under the **Forms** tab, the same as forms signed on the iPad.
- The message is a fixed one from the salon. Texts and email wait in the outbox for now.
