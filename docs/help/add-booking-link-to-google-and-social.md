Put your booking link where clients find you, so they can book in a tap. Each place gets its own link, so you can see which brings bookings.

1. Go to **Settings** and tap **Online booking**.
2. Under **Booking links for Google and social**, find the place: **Google Business Profile**, **Instagram**, **Facebook** or **Your website**.
3. Tap **Copy link** next to it.
4. Tap **How to add it** to see the steps for that place.
5. Paste the link there (for example as your Book button) and save.
6. For a booking form inside your own website, go to **Your booking site** and tap **Copy snippet** under **Embed on your website**, then give it to whoever runs your website.

## Good to know

- Use each place's own link, not the plain one, or Reports can't tell where the booking came from.
- The plain **Booking link** at the top has its own **Copy link** and an **Open** link to see your site.
- Turn online booking on first; while it's off, the link tells clients to call.
- To see the results, see See where online bookings came from.
