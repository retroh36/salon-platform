Once the event's over and paid, complete it so the team's work goes into payroll.

1. Go to **Events** and tap the event.
2. Check **Left to pay** under **Payments** is $0.
3. Tap **Complete event**, then **Yes, complete it**.

## Good to know

- Completing makes one sale for the event. Each person's package counts for their artists (split between hair and makeup by time), so it earns commission like any service.
- The team's share of the service charge and of the travel fee goes to them as tips: the service charge by how much of the work each did, travel evenly. The shares are set on **Settings → Bridal and events**.
- Every payment the event took is on the sale, so it shows on the day's sales.
- Completed events keep their blocks on the calendar as a record of the day.

## If it doesn't work

- "Collect everything first": a payment is still due. See **Payments**.
- No **Complete event** button: the event has to be booked, and its day has to have come.
