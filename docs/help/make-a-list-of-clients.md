A client report lists the clients who match what you choose: what they had, with whom and when, their last and next visit, and spend. It has the same columns every time.

1. Go to Clients, then **Client reports**. Or ask Denise in your own words, such as "Hair clients with 3+ visits since June 2024 who haven't been in this year", and tap **Open the report** under her answer.
2. Choose the visits to count: categories or services, stylists, the dates they count from and until, and how many.
3. Choose their last and next visit if it matters: **No visit since**, **A visit since**, and **Next visit** booked or not.
4. Tap **Run report**.
5. To keep it, tap **Save report** and give it a name. Saved reports are listed at the foot of the page.

## Good to know

- Dates like "Start of this year" or "6 months ago" are kept as words, so a saved report run next year counts from next year.
- Stylists and assistants see only their own clients, and no spend.
- Owners and managers can tick **Share with the team** when saving, so everyone sees it in their list. Each person running it sees only what they're allowed to.
- **Export CSV** downloads the list for those who can export the salon's data. Every download is logged in the Audit trail.
- Denise turns your question into the report's filters. Our own code finds the clients, so the list is the same whoever asks it and however it's worded.
