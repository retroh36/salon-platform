Give one of your days to a coworker in the same role, or trade it for one of theirs. They accept, then a manager approves it.

1. On **My day** or your **Schedule** page, tap **Swap a shift**.
2. Under **Ask for a swap**, choose the **Coworker**, then **Your day to give**.
3. To trade, choose **Their day you take**. Leave it on **None, just give my day** to give yours away.
4. Add a **Note** if you like, then tap **Ask** with their name.
5. When someone asks you, it's under **Asked of you**: tap **Accept** or **Say no**.

## Good to know

- Swaps are for the front desk and assistants, with someone in the same role. Stylists ask a manager to change their hours.
- A swap that would put someone on two shifts at once, or working through approved time off, can't be asked or approved. The form says why first.
- Nothing changes until a manager approves it. Then both schedules show the new hours, and you both get a notice.
- You can **Cancel** a swap you asked for until it's approved. Every step is logged.
