Until you go live, every text and email Maestra writes waits in the outbox and nothing reaches a client. Going live sends them for real.

1. Go to **Settings** and tap **Messages**.
2. Under **Texts and email**, check that **Texts** or **Email** says **Connected**.
3. Under **Send a test to yourself**, choose **Text** or **Email**, type your own number or email, and tap **Send test**.
4. Check your phone or inbox.
5. Tap **Go live**.
6. To stop sending to clients at any time, tap **Stop sending to clients**.

## Good to know

- Only owners can go live, and every change is logged with who made it.
- Only messages written after you go live are sent. Nothing waiting in the outbox from before goes out.
- A client who texted STOP never gets another text, and an email address that bounced or marked you as spam never gets another email.
- The demo and test copies of Maestra never send real messages, even with **Go live** on.

## If it doesn't work

- **Go live** is greyed out: the reason is just under it, for example texts and email aren't connected yet.
- The test went to the outbox: this copy of Maestra doesn't send real messages, or that channel isn't connected yet.
