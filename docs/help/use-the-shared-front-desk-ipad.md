On the salon's shared iPad, switch to yourself with a PIN instead of signing out and in. It locks itself when nobody's using it.

1. Set your PIN once: tap your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open **Your account**, enter a **PIN** twice (6 digits for owners and managers, 4 to 6 for everyone else) and tap **Save PIN**.
2. On the shared iPad, tap **Switch user** at the bottom of the sidebar.
3. On **Who's at the desk?**, tap your name.
4. Enter your PIN and tap **Unlock**. The **Calendar** opens (**My day** for stylists and assistants).

## Good to know

- An owner or manager makes an iPad shared: **Settings**, then **Shared devices**, then **Use this device as a shared device**. The same page sets how long it waits before locking (5 minutes to start) and removes a device.
- Easy PINs like 1234 or 1111 aren't allowed.
- An owner or manager with a shorter PIN from before can't switch in with it: sign in with your password and set a 6-digit one.
- Every switch, and every wrong PIN, is logged.
- Forgot your PIN? Sign in with your password and set a new one, or ask a manager to tap **Clear PIN** on your Team page.

## If it doesn't work

- "Too many wrong PINs": wait 5 minutes, or sign in with your password.
- Your name isn't on the screen: set a PIN first, and make sure you work at this location.
