Book the client's next visit before they leave, right from checkout.

1. Open the checkout for the visit.
2. On the right, below the payments, Denise suggests a time, based on how often they usually come and their usual stylist.
3. Tap **Book it** to book that time.
4. Or tap **Pick another time**, choose a **Day**, and tap one of the free times.
5. You'll see **Rebooked for …**. Tap **Undo** if it's wrong.
6. Finish the payment and tap **Complete sale**. The success screen shows the new visit too.

## Good to know

- The suggestion books the same main services with the same stylist, and follows all the booking rules.
- With no visit history, Denise suggests about six weeks out.
- Rebooking only shows when you're checking out a visit, not a walk-in sale.
- Reports show how many clients rebooked at checkout, per stylist.

## If it doesn't work

- "That time was just taken. Pick another.": someone booked it a moment ago; choose another time.
- "Nothing free that day with this stylist.": try another day.
