Reports show what the salon owes in services, gift card activity, memberships, promotions, bridal and events, where online bookings came from, rebooking, and the day's close-out.

1. In the sidebar, under **Sales**, tap **Reports**.
2. Choose **This month**, **Last month** or **Last 30 days** at the top of the page. Under the title it says which dates you're seeing; every report follows it except what you owe (always as of now) and the close-out (by day).
3. Read **What you owe in services**: gift card balances, unused package uses and promotional credit.
4. Read **Gift cards sold and used**: sold, reloaded and used in the period.
5. Read **Memberships**: members, monthly recurring revenue, who joined and churn.
6. Read **Promotions**: how often each was used, the revenue from those sales, what the discounts cost, openings filled by last-minute specials, and first visits.
7. Read **Bridal and events**: the value of events held in the period and the average event, how many inquiries that came in were booked, deposits collected, deposits waiting on proposals, what's still to collect, and **By month** for six months either side.
8. Read **Where online bookings came from**: bookings, how many came in, and revenue by the link clients followed.
9. Read **Rebooked at checkout**: how many clients left with their next visit booked, by stylist.
10. At the bottom, **Close out the day** shows the day's money by tender. See "Close out the day".

## Good to know

- Owners and managers see Reports (an owner can also allow someone else).
- For each person's sales, commission and tips, see "Run payroll for a pay period". For a day's sales, see **Sales**.
- Memberships run on a test payment provider for now, so no real money moves.
- **Rewards** (when the program is on): points earned, redeemed and expired, the redemption rate, points outstanding with about what they'd come to in rewards (an estimate, not money owed), clients by tier and referrals.
