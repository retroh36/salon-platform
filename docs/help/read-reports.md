Reports show what the salon owes in services, gift card activity, memberships, promotions, where online bookings came from, rebooking, and the day's close-out.

1. In the sidebar, under **Sales**, tap **Reports**.
2. Choose **This month**, **Last month** or **Last 30 days** at the top of the page. Under the title it says which dates you're seeing; every report follows it except what you owe (always as of now) and the close-out (by day).
3. Read **What you owe in services**: gift card balances, unused package uses and promotional credit.
4. Read **Gift cards sold and used**: sold, reloaded and used in the period.
5. Read **Memberships**: members, monthly recurring revenue, who joined and churn.
6. Read **Promotions**: how often each was used, the revenue from those sales, what the discounts cost, openings filled by last-minute specials, and first visits.
7. Read **Where online bookings came from**: bookings, how many came in, and revenue by the link clients followed.
8. Read **Rebooked at checkout**: how many clients left with their next visit booked, by stylist.
9. At the bottom, **Close out the day** shows the day's money by tender. See "Close out the day".

## Good to know

- Owners and managers see Reports (an owner can also allow someone else).
- For each person's sales, commission and tips, see "Run payroll for a pay period". For a day's sales, see **Sales**.
- Memberships run on a test payment provider for now, so no real money moves.
