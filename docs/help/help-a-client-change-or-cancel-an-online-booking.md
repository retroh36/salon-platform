Clients who booked online can move or cancel their own visit from the page they saw after booking, up to your cutoff.

1. The client opens the link from their booking confirmation. It shows **Your booking**: date, time, services, stylists and total.
2. To move it, they tap **Reschedule**, pick a date and time under **Pick a new time**, then tap **Move my booking**.
3. To cancel, they tap **Cancel booking**, read the policy, then tap **Yes, cancel it** (or **Keep it**).
4. The change shows on the calendar by itself.

## Good to know

- After the cutoff (set by the owner in **Cancel or change online until (hours before)**), the page says changes are by phone. Make the change for them on the calendar.
- Texts and email aren't connected yet, so the confirmation with this link doesn't reach clients for now. They can still call, and you can change the booking on the calendar.
- A moved booking keeps its services; the page also has **Book another visit**.
- Online bookings show a globe in booking details' **History** tab.
- A cancellation or a new booking by a client never comes from Denise: she never messages clients.
