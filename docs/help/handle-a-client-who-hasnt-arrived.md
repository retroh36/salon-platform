When a client is 15 minutes past their start time and not checked in, Denise lists them under **Not arrived yet**.

1. Open Denise's panel (tap her face under the calendar, or the **Denise** tab).
2. Find the **Not arrived yet** card. Each row shows the client's name and when they were due.
3. Give them a call. Tap **Open** to see their booking, where you can tap the phone icon next to their name.
4. If they're on the way: in booking details, tap **⋯**, then **Running late**, and say how late.
5. If they're not coming: tap **Mark no-show** in their row, then **Yes, no-show**. (Tap **Undo** on the message that follows if it was a mistake.)
6. If there's nothing to do: tap the × in the corner of their row to dismiss it.

## Good to know

- On the calendar, a client who's due shows a clock with how late they are ("+5m"), then "Not arrived" from 15 minutes.
- A dismissed row stays gone for the rest of the day, for everyone at the salon.
- Stylists can see the card but only the front desk, managers and owners can mark a no-show or dismiss.
