Cancel a client's package and refund what the unused uses are worth.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Clients** and open the client.
2. Find the package under **Packages**.
3. Tap **Cancel package**.
4. Check the amount shown: what the uses left are worth.
5. Under **Refund**, choose back to their card, in cash, or **No refund**.
6. Enter a **Reason**.
7. Tap **Cancel package**. (Tap **Keep it** to change your mind.)

## Good to know

- The refund is recorded on the sale that sold the package.
- Card refunds are recorded as given on your salon's own card terminal, so give the refund there too.
- A canceled package can't be used again.
