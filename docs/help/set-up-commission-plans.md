Set how people are paid: commission at a flat rate or in tiers, hourly, or booth rent, with an optional back-bar fee.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Settings**, then **Commission**.
2. Under **Add a plan**, enter a **Plan name** and choose **How they're paid**: **Commission**, **Hourly** or **Booth rent**.
3. For commission, enter the **Services %** and **Retail %**. For tiers, tap **Add a services tier** (or retail), enter where it starts and its rate, and choose whether **Tiers start again** each pay period or each month.
4. To charge a **Back-bar fee**, choose a set amount or a percent of each service.
5. For booth rent, enter the **Rent ($)** and whether it's due every week or every month.
6. Tick **Default for new team members** if new people should start on it, then tap **Add plan**.
7. Under **Who's on which plan**, choose each person's plan. "Own rates" means their own rates from Team → Schedule.

## Good to know

- Tiers pay each dollar at the rate of the tier it falls in. With 40% to $4,000, then 45%, a $200 sale at $3,900 earns $40 plus $45, so reaching a tier never costs anyone.
- The back-bar fee comes off each service before commission; it never applies to retail.
- Booth renters keep their services and retail, less any back-bar fee, and their rent comes off their pay, shared out by the day.
- Hourly plans pay no service commission. Hours are paid at each person's own rate.
- Changing a plan only counts for sales from then on. Each sale keeps the plan it was made on, so past pay never changes.
- A plan can be removed once nobody is on it.
