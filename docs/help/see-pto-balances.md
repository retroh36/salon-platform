See what everyone has earned, used and has left, and adjust a balance.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Scheduling**, then the **Balances** tab.
2. Each person shows **Carried over**, **Earned this year**, **Used this year**, **Balance**, **Booked**, **Waiting** and **Left to ask for**.
3. Under it, **Time off taken this PTO year** shows each person's hours by type, including types that don't use PTO.
4. Tap a name for their statement: every grant, hour earned, day used and carry-over, with the balance after each.
5. To adjust it, choose **Add** or **Take off**, enter the **Hours**, when it **Counts from** and the **Reason**, then tap **Save adjustment**.
6. Tap **Export CSV** for everyone's balances in a spreadsheet.

## Good to know

- **Left to ask for** is the balance less time off already approved for later.
- Every adjustment is kept with who made it and why, in the Audit trail (PTO balances).
- Only those who manage the team can adjust a balance, and a manager can't change an owner's.
- Each person sees their own balance on their Time off page.
- You can also ask Denise "How many sick days has Nola taken this year?" or "Who's off next week?". Owners and managers get anyone's answer; everyone else only their own.
