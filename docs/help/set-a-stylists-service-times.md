Give a stylist their own length for each service they do, by hand or copied from another stylist and then adjusted.

1. Open **Team** and tap the stylist's name.
2. Tap **Service times**.
3. To start from another stylist's times, choose them in **Copy from** and tap **Copy their times**. Nothing is saved yet.
4. Change any minutes under **Active**, **Processing** and **Finishing**. **Use menu time** puts a service back to the menu's length.
5. Tap **Save**.

## Good to know

- Copying takes the other stylist's time for every service both of them do, including where they use the menu's time. Services only this stylist does stay as they were.
- New bookings with the stylist use their times. Visits already booked keep their own length.
- Times are in steps of 5 minutes, at least 5 minutes active and 8 hours at most. Finishing time needs processing time before it.
- A time the same as the menu's is kept as the menu's, so a later change to the menu reaches them.
- Denise suggests lengths from real visits on **Durations**.
- You can also ask Denise, such as "Change Hannah's blowout to 30 minutes" or "Copy Bridget's times to Shae". She shows each change, before and after, and nothing changes until you tap **Confirm** under her answer. After that, **Undo** puts the times back.
- Denise's **Confirm** only works while the times are still what she saw. If someone changed them since, ask her again. **Undo** stops working once the times are changed again.

## If it doesn't work

- **Save** is greyed out: a time breaks one of the rules above, and the row says which.
- "They no longer do one of these services": their services changed since the page opened. Reload it.
