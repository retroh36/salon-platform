Take money off one line or the whole sale, with a reason.

1. Open the checkout for the visit or sale.
2. **One line:** tap the line, and under **Discount on this line** choose **Percent off** or **Amount off**, type the amount, and type why in **Why (e.g. loyalty)**.
3. **The whole sale:** under the lines, tap **Discount the whole sale**.
4. Choose **Percent off** or **Amount off**, type the amount and the **Reason**.
5. Tap **Apply discount**. (To remove it later, tap **Change discount**, clear the amount and tap **No discount**.)
6. Check the **Discounts** line in the totals, then take payment.

## Good to know

- Every discount needs a reason.
- Who can discount is set by the owner in Settings, under **Tax and receipts**. Owners always can; managers and the front desk only if turned on.
- The front desk can take off up to a set percent of the sale; managers and owners have no limit.
- Member prices come off by themselves and don't count against the limit.
- Gift cards, and services paid with a package or membership, can't be discounted.

## If it doesn't work

- "You can give up to …% off. Ask a manager for more." or "You can't give discounts. Ask a manager.": ask a manager or owner to finish the checkout.
