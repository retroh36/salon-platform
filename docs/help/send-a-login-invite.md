Email someone on the team a link to choose a password and sign in.

1. In the sidebar, under **Salon**, tap **Team**, then tap their name.
2. Check their **Email** (add it and tap **Save changes** if it's missing).
3. Under **Login**, tap **Send invite**.
4. They open the email, tap the button and choose a password. They're then signed in.
5. If they haven't signed in yet (**Invited**), tap **Send the invite again** for a fresh link.

## Good to know

- The link works once, for an hour.
- Managers can invite anyone but owners.
- Deactivated people can't be invited; reactivate them first.
