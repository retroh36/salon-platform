Take payment for a client's visit on the checkout screen.

1. On the calendar, tap the visit and tap **Check out** at the bottom of booking details. The checkout screen opens with the visit's services on the ticket.
2. Add anything else: search **Add a product** or tap a tile to add retail, or tap **Add service** under the lines.
3. Tap a line to change its price (with a reason), quantity, stylist or discount.
4. Under **Tips · on each stylist's services**, tap 18%, 20% or 25%, or enter a custom tip for each stylist.
5. Under **Payment**, tap **Card**, **Cash**, **Gift card** or **Package**, check the amount and record it.
6. When it says **Paid in full**, tap **Complete sale**.
7. Choose a receipt (print, email or none), and rebook them if Denise suggests a time.

## Good to know

- The ticket saves as you go: tap **Back** and nothing is lost.
- Card payments are taken on the salon's own card terminal; here you only record them (last 4 digits or approval code).
- To pay part by card and part by cash, see Split a payment. To let the client pay on their phone, see Send a payment link.

## If it doesn't work

- **Complete sale** is greyed out: the reason is shown just above it, such as an amount still to pay or a discount over the limit.
