When someone leaves, deactivate them: they can't sign in or be booked, and everything they did stays.

1. In the sidebar, under **Salon**, tap **Team**, then tap their name.
2. Under **Leaving the salon**, tap **Deactivate** and their name.
3. Read the note: if they still have visits booked, move them to someone else first.
4. Tap **Deactivate** to confirm.
5. If they come back, open their page and tap **Reactivate**.

## Good to know

- Nobody is ever deleted, so their visits, sales, commission and pay history stay.
- You can't deactivate yourself, managers can't deactivate owners, and the salon always keeps at least one owner.
