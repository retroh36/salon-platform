Count the cash drawer at the end of the day and check it against the day's sales.

1. Go to **Reports** and scroll to **Close out the day**. It opens on today (use **← Previous day** for an earlier day).
2. Check the table: what was taken, refunded and net for each way of paying.
3. Check **Starting float ($)**: the cash the drawer started with today. It's filled in with your usual float; change it if today's was different.
4. Count the cash in the drawer. Compare with **Cash expected in the drawer** (the float plus the day's cash).
5. Type the total in **Cash counted ($)**, and add a **Note (optional)** to explain any difference.
6. Tap **Save count**. It says whether the drawer matches, or how much it's over or short.

## Good to know

- Owners and managers see Reports and close out the day (an owner can also allow someone else).
- Expected cash is the starting float plus the day's cash payments, less cash refunds and voids.
- Set the usual float on Settings, under **Tax and receipts** (**Cash drawer**). A float changed for one day stays with that day.
- You can save the count again for the same day; the latest count replaces the earlier one.
- For the day's totals by payment type without counting, see Find a past sale (the **Taken** card on Sales).
