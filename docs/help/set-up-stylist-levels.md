Choose your salon's stylist levels: their names, which is most senior, and each one's color and badge.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Settings**, then **Levels**.
2. To change the order, drag a level by its grip or use its up and down arrows. Put the most senior level first.
3. To change a level, tap **Edit** beside it. Change its **Name**, then pick a **Color** and a **Badge**. Tap **Done**.
4. To add one, tap **Add a level**, give it a name, and pick a color and badge.
5. Tap **Save levels**.

## Good to know

- A salon can have up to 8 levels, and at least one.
- **Remove level** only works when nobody is on that level. Move them to another level first (see "Set a stylist's level").
- Each level can have its own price for a service. See "Set prices by level for a service".
- The color shows as a ring and badge on the stylist's photo, a bar along the top of their calendar column, and on the booking site. The badge's symbol shows the level too, so it's never color alone.
- Denise's schedule checks look for your most senior level ("No Master stylist working").
- Owners and managers can change levels.
