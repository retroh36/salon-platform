Forgive a client's first no-show once in a while, and tell them so in your own words. Every fee after that is charged as usual.

1. Go to **Settings** and tap **Policies**.
2. Under **Grace**, tick **Give clients grace for their first missed visit**.
3. Under **For**, tick **No-shows**, **Late cancellations** or both.
4. Type how often a client can have grace in **Once every (months)**.
5. Under **The fee**, choose **Waive it straight away**, or **Waive it if they rebook with the same stylist within** a number of days.
6. Write the **Message to the client** and check **How it reads**.
7. Tap **Save grace**.

## Good to know

- The fee is waived by itself, but the message never goes by itself. Booking details and Denise's card ask "Let Ava know their fee was waived?" with the message as it will read, and **Send** or **Don't send**. Nothing goes out without **Send**.
- If your role can write its own messages, you can change the words before you send. A client who doesn't take messages about visits shows **No messages: call**, with **Done** once you've called.
- Until someone sends it or chooses **Don't send**, the visit, the client's profile and the Policy page show **Grace message not sent**.
- The message can use {first_name}, {salon}, {stylist}, {by} (the last day to rebook) and {booking_link}. It goes on the client's channel for visit messages, never to a number that replied STOP, and Denise never writes it.
- While a fee waits for a rebooking, booking details and the Policy page show "waived if they rebook with Gil by Fri, Nov 20". Booking with that stylist in time waives it; otherwise it's charged when the time's up.
- Taking the no-show back takes the grace back too, so the client still has it next time.
- Waived fees don't count toward repeat offenders.
