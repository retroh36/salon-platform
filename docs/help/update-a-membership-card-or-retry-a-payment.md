Change the card a membership renews on, or try a failed payment again.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Clients** and open the client.
2. Find their membership under **Membership**. A failed payment shows as past due, with when it'll be tried next.
3. To change the card, tap **Update card**, choose the **New card**, and tap **Save card**.
4. To try a failed payment again on the same card, tap **Try the payment again**.
5. Read the message: it says whether the payment went through.

## Good to know

- Payments are in test mode for now: you choose a test card and no real card is saved or charged.
- If the payment failed, saving a new card tries it again straight away.
- While a payment has failed there are no member benefits.
- Failed payments are tried again by themselves after 1, 3 and 4 more days, with a notice to owners and managers each time. After the fourth failure the membership ends.
- Failed payments also show on the Dashboard.
