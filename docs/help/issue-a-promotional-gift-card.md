Give a client a free card for a referral, an apology or an offer, tracked apart from cards people bought.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Gift cards**.
2. Find **Issue a promotional card**.
3. Enter the **Amount ($)**.
4. Check **Lasts (days)**: it's filled in with your salon's default.
5. In **What it's for**, say why (for example "Referral reward").
6. Optionally add who it's **For (name, optional)** and their **Email (optional)**.
7. Tap **Issue card**. The new card opens with its code.
8. To send it, use **Send it** on the card and tap **Email card**.

## Good to know

- Promotional cards have no cash value: they pay for services and products but are never refunded as money.
- They always expire, and they can't be reloaded.
- They're listed under the **Promotional** tab on Gift cards.
- Emailed cards wait in the outbox for now: email isn't connected yet.
- Owners set the default number of days on Settings, Gift cards.
