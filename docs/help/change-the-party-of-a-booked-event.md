Add or take someone off a booked event. Until the headcount cutoff, everything updates by itself.

1. Go to **Events**, tap the event and tap **Edit event**.
2. Under **Party**, tap **+ Bridesmaid** (or another button) to add someone, or **×** to take them off.
3. Check the new **Total** and the **Timeline**.
4. Tap **Save changes**.

## Good to know

- The total, the payments still to come, the timeline and the team's blocks on the calendar all follow the new party. What's already paid stays paid.
- The cutoff date is under the **Total**: "Party changes until …". It's set on **Settings → Bridal and events**.
- After the cutoff, only an owner or manager can change the party.

## If it doesn't work

- "The headcount cutoff has passed": ask an owner or manager to make the change.
- Someone on the team isn't free for the new timeline: choose someone else or move the timeline.
