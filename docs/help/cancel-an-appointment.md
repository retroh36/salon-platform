Cancel a visit and record why.

1. On the calendar, tap the visit to open booking details.
2. Tap **⋯** (More actions) at the bottom, then **Cancel booking**.
3. Choose a **Reason**: Client canceled, Client rescheduled, Client is sick, Stylist unavailable, Salon closed or Other.
4. Add **Details** if you like (needed for **Other**).
5. For a repeating or group visit, choose **This visit only**, **The whole group**, **This and following visits** or **All visits in the series**.
6. Tap **Cancel booking** (or **Keep it** to go back).

## Good to know

- The visit leaves the calendar. Denise then looks at the time that opened up and may suggest waitlist clients who fit it.
- Booked, confirmed and checked-in visits can be canceled. A checked-out visit can't; a manager or owner voids its sale instead.
- Clients who booked online can also cancel themselves from their confirmation link, until the salon's cutoff.
- If the client just didn't come, use Mark a no-show instead.
