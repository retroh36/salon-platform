If a client asks for no more offers or reminders, untick their consent on their profile.

1. Go to **Clients** and search for the client by name or phone.
2. Open their profile and scroll to **Details**.
3. Untick **Texts OK (reminders and offers)** and/or **Marketing emails OK**.
4. Tap **Save changes**.

## Good to know

- Automated messages only go to clients with one of these ticked. Tick them only when the client has said yes.
- Every automated message has its own opt-out link (**Stop texts** or **Unsubscribe**). When a client uses it, that box is unticked for them by itself.
- Opting out stops marketing only; messages about bookings they make still come.
- Texts aren't connected yet, so replying STOP to a text doesn't work yet; untick the box for them instead.
- Stylists can see a client's choice (**Messages** on the profile) but can't change it.
