Correct the stock of one product, for breakage, samples or mistakes.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Inventory**.
2. Tap the product.
3. Under **Adjust**, choose **Remove** or **Add**.
4. Enter the **Amount** (in grams, ml or units, as the product is measured).
5. Under **Why**, say what happened, for example "Dropped a tube".
6. Tap **Adjust**. You'll see "Stock adjusted."

## Good to know

- Every change is listed under **History** on the product, with the reason.
- To count everything on the shelves, use a stocktake instead. See "Do a stocktake".
- Sales, service use and deliveries change stock by themselves; you don't need to adjust for them.
- Front desk and stylists can see stock but can't adjust it.
