Send the client a link to the party, prices, payment schedule and terms. They book by signing and paying the deposit.

1. Go to **Events** and tap the event.
2. Under **Proposal**, choose **Text** or **Email** and check the number or address.
3. Tap **Send proposal**. The event moves to **Proposal sent**.
4. The client opens the link, types their full name to sign, ticks that they agree and pays the deposit.
5. The event moves to **Booked**. **Agreement** shows who signed, when, and the terms version. Tap **What they agreed to** to see the exact words.

## Good to know

- If the client's connection drops as they tap, their page says so and asks them to try again. They're never charged twice: if it went through, trying again says so.
- Before you send, **Proposal** shows the payment schedule as it would be if they booked today.
- The link works for 14 days. Tap **Send it again** for a new link. The old one stops working.
- If you change the event after sending, the client sees the new version. If they had the page open, they're asked to reload it before they can sign.
- The card they pay the deposit with is kept for the later payments, as the terms say.
- The deposit is non-refundable unless an owner changes that on Settings, **Bridal and events**.

## If it doesn't work

- "Add the client and the party before sending a proposal": choose the client and add the party in **Edit event**.
- "doesn't get texts from the salon": send it by email instead.
