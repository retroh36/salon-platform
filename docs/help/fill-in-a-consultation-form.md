Have a client fill in and sign a form on the iPad at the front desk.

1. Open the client's profile, tap the **Forms** tab and tap **Fill in a form**. Or, in booking details, tap **Before this visit** and then **Fill it in now** next to the form.
2. If asked, choose the form from the list.
3. Hand the iPad to the client. They answer each question and sign in the signature box (**Clear signature** to start again).
4. They tap **Sign and submit**.
5. When it says "Please hand the iPad back to the front desk", take it back and tap **Done**.

## Good to know

- Questions marked with * must be answered. If any are missing, the form lists what's still needed.
- Signed forms are kept on the client's profile under the **Forms** tab. Tap one to see it.
- A signed form can't be changed. To correct it, fill in a new one.
- Owners create the forms and choose when they're needed (see Set up a consultation form).
