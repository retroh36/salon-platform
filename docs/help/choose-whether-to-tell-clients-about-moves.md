When someone drags or edits a visit's time, stylist or date, the calendar asks whether to let the client know. Choose which answer comes first.

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Messages**.
2. Under **When a visit is moved**, choose **Usually send** or **Usually don't**.
3. It saves by itself.

## Good to know

- It only decides which button is first. Nothing goes to a client until someone taps **Send**.
- Busy day? Anyone can tick **Don't ask again today** on the question. The calendar stops asking on that device until tomorrow, and clients moved then aren't told.
- A client who takes no messages about visits shows **No messages: call**, with their number.
- Clients who book or change a visit online still get their usual confirmation, since they started it.
- Denise's fixes ask the same way: **Message ready to send** after Apply.
