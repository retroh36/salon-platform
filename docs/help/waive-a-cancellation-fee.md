Waive a fee before it's charged, or after (it's refunded), with a reason.

1. Open the visit in booking details, the client's profile, or **Policy** in the sidebar.
2. Find the fee and tap **Waive…** (or **Waive and refund…** once charged).
3. Type why you're waiving it.
4. Tap **Waive fee** (or **Waive and refund**).

## Good to know

- Every waiver is logged with who did it and why.
- A waived fee doesn't count toward repeat offenders.
- The owner chooses who can waive on Policy settings; the front desk can only if it's ticked there.
