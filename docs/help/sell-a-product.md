Sell retail products to someone without a visit to check out.

1. Go to **Sales** and tap **New sale**. (Or, on a product's page in **Inventory**, tap **Sell this product**.)
2. Optional: type in **Find a client** and pick them, so the sale is on their profile and you can email the receipt.
3. Search **Add a product: name, brand, SKU or scan**, or scan the barcode, and tap the product. Or tap a tile under **Best sellers**.
4. To credit a stylist with the sale, choose them in **Credit to** before adding products.
5. Tap a line to change the **Quantity**, or tap **×** to remove it.
6. Under **Payment**, tap **Card** or **Cash** and record the payment.
7. Tap **Complete sale**, then choose a receipt.

## Good to know

- Without a client, the sale is a **Walk-in sale**.
- Products come out of stock when the sale completes.
- If a product shows "Only 0 in stock", see Sell a product that's out of stock.
- Stylists credited with retail earn their retail commission on it.
