Sell retail products, gift cards, packages or memberships to someone without a visit to check out.

1. Tap **New sale**: at the top of the calendar, or on **Sales**. (Or, on a product's page in **Inventory**, tap **Sell this product**.)
2. Type in **Find a client** and pick them, or tap **Walk-in guest** when there's no client.
3. Check **Credit to**: it starts on you. Choose the stylist who should earn the retail commission before adding products.
4. Search **Add a product: name, brand, SKU or scan**, or scan the barcode, and tap the product. Or tap a tile under **Best sellers**.
5. Tap a line to change the **Quantity** or who it's credited to, or tap **×** to remove it.
6. Add a gift card, package or membership, a promotion or a reward with the buttons under the lines.
7. Under **Payment**, tap **Card** or **Cash** and record the payment, then tap **Complete sale** and choose a receipt.

## Good to know

- Packages, memberships and rewards need a client; a walk-in guest can buy products and gift cards.
- Products come out of stock when the sale completes, and the sale shows in Sales and Reports like any other.
- If a product shows "Only 0 in stock", see Sell a product that's out of stock.
