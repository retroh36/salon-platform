Move between days, zoom in or out, and filter the calendar.

1. Go to **Calendar**. Today opens with the current time about a third of the way down.
2. Use the arrows (**Previous day**, **Next day**), **Today**, or tap the date to pick any day.
3. Choose a **Zoom**: **Compact**, **Comfortable** or **Detailed**. On an iPad you can also pinch the calendar. Your choice is remembered.
4. To see only one kind of service, pick a department (for example **Color** or **Cuts**) instead of **All departments**.
5. Tap a visit to open booking details. Tap **Close panel** (×), press Escape, or tap empty space to close it.

## Good to know

- Only the **Day** view works for now. **Week** is coming soon.
- Each column header shows the stylist's hours and a summary of their day; hover for the full text.
- Stylists who are off with nothing booked fold into a thin column. Tap it to show them.
- The calendar refreshes by itself when someone else, or an online booking, changes it.
- To learn what the colors mean, see Read the calendar colors.
