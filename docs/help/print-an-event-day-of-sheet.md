Print, or save as a PDF, one page with everything the team needs on the day.

1. Open the event: tap it on **Events**, or tap its block on the calendar.
2. Tap **Day-of sheet**.
3. Tap **Print or save as PDF**. To save a PDF, choose "Save as PDF" in the print window.

## Good to know

- It shows where and when, the client to call, each artist's leave time (on location), call time and when they're done, the running order, the party, and the look.
- It's made from the event as saved, so print it again after any change.
- If an artist isn't chosen yet, the sheet says so in red.
- Stylists on the event see the sheet and the look, but not what the event costs or what's been paid: that's for the front desk, managers and owners.
