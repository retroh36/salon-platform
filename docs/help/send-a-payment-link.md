Text (or email) the client a link so they can tip and pay on their own phone.

1. Open the checkout and finish the ticket: lines, discounts and price changes. Don't record any payment.
2. Under **Payment**, tap **Text a payment link instead**.
3. Choose **Text** or **Email** and check the **Mobile number** or **Email**.
4. Tap **Send payment link**.
5. The checkout shows **Waiting for payment**. When the client pays, the sale completes by itself and shows **Paid by link**.

## Good to know

- The client must be on the sale, and nothing can be paid yet.
- The link works once and expires after 24 hours.
- While it's out, the ticket can't be changed. To change it or pay at the desk, see Cancel a payment link.
- Texts and emails aren't connected yet: for now the link waits in the outbox and isn't actually delivered.
- To explain the client's side, see Help a client pay by link.
