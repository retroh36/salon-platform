An assistant sees the day and clients of the stylist they help, without prices or pay, and can't book or check anyone in.

1. Add them on **Team** with **Add team member**, and choose **Assistant** as their **Role**.
2. On their page, under **Assisting**, choose the **Stylist** they help.
3. It saves as you choose. To move them to someone else, choose another stylist.

## Good to know

- On **My day** they see that stylist's day as it's booked. The stylist still taps their own times.
- They see the clients on that stylist's book only, and their own pay on **My day**.
- Choose **Nobody yet** to take them off; they then see no one's book.
