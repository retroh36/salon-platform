Set when the salon is open each week. Staff can only be booked while the salon is open.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Team**, then the **Schedule** tab.
2. Tap **Opening hours** at the top right.
3. On a closed day, tap **Add hours**, then set the opening and closing times.
4. To close for part of the day, tap **Add split** and set the second part.
5. To close a whole day, tap **Remove** next to its hours.
6. Tap **Save hours**.

## Good to know

- Hours are in the salon's own time zone, shown at the top of the page.
- Online booking only offers times when the salon is open and the stylist is working.
- Everyone can see the opening hours; only managers and owners can change them.
