Book several people as one party, all at once or one after another.

1. Tap an empty time on the calendar (or **New booking**), then tap **Book a group instead**.
2. Give it a **Group name** (for example "Ava's bridal party").
3. Under **Timing**, set **First starts at**, then choose **Everyone at the same time**, **Staggered, every** some minutes, or **Set each guest's time**.
4. For each guest, find the client (or leave it empty for a guest without a record), choose the **Service** and **Stylist**.
5. Tap **+ Add a guest** for more people (up to 12).
6. Add **Notes for everyone (optional)**, then tap **Book** (it shows the number, for example **Book 4 guests**).

## Good to know

- Everyone is booked together or no one is. If one guest doesn't fit, nothing is booked and that guest's problem is shown.
- In booking details, a group visit lists the other guests; tap a name to open their visit.
- Dragging or canceling a group visit asks whether it's for this visit only or the whole group.
