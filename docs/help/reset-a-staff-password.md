Send someone on the team a link to choose a new password.

1. In the sidebar, under **Salon**, tap **Team**, then tap their name.
2. Under **Login**, tap **Send a password reset**.
3. They open the email, tap **Choose a password** and choose a new one.

## Good to know

- Only owners can send a reset from here. Anyone can also tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen themselves.
- The link works once, for an hour. Their old password works until they choose a new one.
- Someone who never signed in gets **Send the invite again** instead.
