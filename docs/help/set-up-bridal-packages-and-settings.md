Choose the packages a wedding party picks from, and how events are priced, paid and planned.

1. Go to **Settings** and tap **Bridal and events**.
2. Under **Event packages**, fill in a **Name**, who it's **Usually for**, the **Hair (minutes)** and **Makeup (minutes)** it takes and the **Price ($)**. Tap **Add package**.
3. Tick **A trial** for a trial package (booked as a visit before the day).
4. To change a package, tap it, change it and tap **Save package**.
5. Under **Bridal settings**, set the deposit, the payment schedule, the headcount cutoff, the service charge, travel, early starts and the timeline.
6. Edit the **Contract terms** clients agree to.
7. Tap **Save settings**.

## Good to know

- The minutes say who can do a package: hair, makeup or both. Who does event hair and makeup is set on each person's page in **Team** (**Event work**).
- Changing a package's price doesn't change events already set up.
- The contract terms are a starter version. Have your own lawyer review them before you use them. Each change is kept, so every client's agreement shows the exact words they agreed to.
- The team's share of the service charge and travel fees is paid with their tips.
