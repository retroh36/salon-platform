When something clashes (a visit running over, a late client, a stylist out, a double-booking), Denise suggests fixes. You decide.

1. Open the **Denise** tab in the calendar's side panel, or tap the Denise button in the calendar's corner (its badge shows how many things are waiting).
2. Read Denise's card: what's wrong and **Why it works** for the best fix. The calendar shows the visits involved glowing, with dashed outlines where they'd move.
3. Tap **Apply fix** to make the change, or **Dismiss** to say no.
4. To see others, tap **Other options**, then **Use this instead** on the one you want.
5. Changed your mind? Tap **Undo** on the message that appears after applying.

## Good to know

- Fixes are ranked: fewest clients moved first, then the smallest delay.
- Denise never moves clients who are checked in or finished, or anyone earlier or into the past.
- Applying a fix checks the booking rules again first. If the day has changed since, Denise says so.
- Clients aren't messaged yet. Let anyone who's moved know yourself.
- Stylists can see the fixes; the front desk, managers and owners apply them.
