When something clashes (a visit running over, a late client, a stylist out, a double-booking), Denise suggests fixes. You decide.

1. Tap Denise's face under the calendar (its badge shows how many things are waiting), or her small bar in the corner if she's minimized. Her card opens in the bottom right corner.
2. Read Denise's card: what's wrong and **Why it works** for the best fix. The calendar shows the visits involved glowing, with dashed outlines where they'd move.
3. Tap **Apply fix** to make the change, or **Dismiss** to say no.
4. To see others, tap **Other options**, then **Use this instead** on the one you want.
5. After applying, Denise says who moved, for example "Moved Grace. Message ready to send." Under it is a **Your visit has moved** message for each client, with their new time. Check it, change the words if you need to, and tap **Send** (or **Send all**). Nobody is told until you send it. After a delay she absorbed, she asks "Let Grace know you're running about 15 minutes behind?" instead: tap **Send** or **Don't send**.
6. Changed your mind? Tap **Undo** on the message that appears after applying. Later on, Undo is still there: under **Fixes applied** on Denise's card, and in the visit's booking details, **History** tab.
7. If the client was already sent the message, Undo asks first: "Grace was told 3:05pm. Undo and send a correction?", with the correction they'd get ("please ignore our last message: your visit is still on for…"). Tap **Undo and write the correction**, check it under **Corrections to send**, and tap **Send**. **Keep it** leaves the fix as it is.

## Good to know

- Fixes are ranked: a short delay the day can absorb first (one client starting up to 20 minutes later with the same stylist, no one else moved), then fewest clients moved, then the smallest delay.
- When Denise sees a visit today likely to run into the next client (the stylist usually takes longer than booked), the stylist hears first: a **Heads up** on their My day. The front desk sees her fix 10 minutes later, or at once if the stylist says they're running late.
- Denise never moves clients who are checked in or finished, or anyone earlier or into the past.
- A client who asked for their stylist (**Asked for** in booking details), or who only sees their preferred stylist, always stays with that stylist: Denise only suggests a later time with them.
- Applying a fix checks the booking rules again first. If the day has changed since, Denise says so.
- Applying a fix never messages anyone. A message shows **Sent** only once it has gone.
- Messages go only the way each client allows for visit messages; if they don't get any, it shows **No messages: call**. Your role may only be able to send the message as it is (Settings → Messages).
- Undo never messages anyone either: the correction waits for you to send it, like the first message.
- The visit's **History** tab lists each fix that moved it ("Moved by Denise's fix, applied by Sam Rivera") and when it was put back.
- On the Dashboard, **Apply** shows the same messages to send.
- Stylists can see the fixes; the front desk, managers and owners apply them.
