When something clashes (a visit running over, a late client, a stylist out, a double-booking), Denise suggests fixes. You decide.

1. Tap Denise's face under the calendar (its badge shows how many things are waiting), or her small bar in the corner if she's minimized. Her card opens in the bottom right corner.
2. Read Denise's card: what's wrong and **Why it works** for the best fix. The calendar shows the visits involved glowing, with dashed outlines where they'd move.
3. Tap **Apply fix** to make the change, or **Dismiss** to say no.
4. To see others, tap **Other options**, then **Use this instead** on the one you want.
5. After applying, Denise says who moved, for example "Moved Grace. Message ready to send." Under it is a **Your visit has moved** message for each client, with their new time. Check it, change the words if you need to, and tap **Send** (or **Send all**). Nobody is told until you send it.
6. Changed your mind? Tap **Undo** on the message that appears after applying.

## Good to know

- Fixes are ranked: fewest clients moved first, then the smallest delay.
- Denise never moves clients who are checked in or finished, or anyone earlier or into the past.
- Applying a fix checks the booking rules again first. If the day has changed since, Denise says so.
- Applying a fix never messages anyone. A message shows **Sent** only once it has gone.
- Messages go only the way each client allows for visit messages; if they don't get any, it shows **No messages: call**. Your role may only be able to send the message as it is (Settings → Messages).
- Already sent and then undone? The client has the first message, so message them again (Message client in booking details).
- On the Dashboard, **Apply** shows the same messages to send.
- Stylists can see the fixes; the front desk, managers and owners apply them.
