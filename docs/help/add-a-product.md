Add a color, back-bar or retail product so you can use it in services or sell it, and track its stock.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Inventory**, then **Add product**.
2. Enter the **Brand**, the **Line** (optional) and the **Shade or name**.
3. Under **Type**, choose **Color (shades for formulas)**, **Back bar (used in services)** or **Retail (sold to clients)**.
4. Under **Measured in**, choose **Grams**, **ml** or **Units**, and enter the container size.
5. Enter the **Cost per container ($)** and, for retail, the **Retail price ($)**.
6. Choose a **Supplier**, and add the **SKU**, **Barcode** and **Shelf** if you have them.
7. Tap **Add product**.

## Good to know

- **Color** is for shades (permanent, demi and toners), measured in grams or ml. Developer, lightener and treatments are **Back bar**. Both go in formulas and recipes; Inventory can show each on its own.
- A new product starts with nothing on hand. Add stock by receiving an order, adjusting stock or doing a stocktake.
- The **Shelf** groups products when you count them in a stocktake.
- To change a product later, open it and tap **Edit product**. Untick **In use** to hide one you no longer stock.
- If the supplier isn't in the list, add it first. See "Add a supplier".
