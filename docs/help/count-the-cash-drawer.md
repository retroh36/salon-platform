Count the drawer when the salon opens, before the first cash of the day, and again whenever you like during the day, for example at a shift change. Maestra works out what should be there and tells you, after you count, whether it matches.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Sales**, then **Cash drawer**.
2. Count the cash in the drawer.
3. Type it under **Cash counted ($)**. Add a note if you like.
4. Tap **Save count**. It says whether the drawer matches, or how much it's over or short.
5. To count again later in the day, do the same under **Count the drawer**.

## Good to know

- The first count of the day is the opening count. Counting it again replaces it.
- The opening count is checked against what last night's close-out left in the drawer, after the bank deposit. If there wasn't one, it's checked against the salon's usual float.
- A count during the day is checked against the opening count plus the day's cash so far, with refunds and voids taken off.
- You aren't told what to expect before you count, so the count is honest.
- When a count is off, owners and managers get an alert. The page lists every cash payment and refund since the count before it, with who took it.
- Cash can't be taken until the opening count is done. Cards, gift cards and other payments aren't held up. At checkout, **Cash** asks for the count right there.
- A count that's over or short only warns: cash can still be taken.
- **Sales** reminds you if the drawer hasn't been counted today.
- Owners and managers close out the day on **Reports**, under **Close out the day**. That's where the bank deposit goes, and **Cash drawer counts** shows every count of the last 30 days.

## Can't count it yet?

- A manager or owner can let today's cash go ahead without the opening count, under **Can't count it yet?** on the Cash drawer page or in the checkout's **Cash**. Type the reason; at the front desk, the manager signs in there with their email and password, just for this.
- Owners and managers get an alert with who allowed it and why, and it's in the **Audit trail** under **Cash drawer**.

## If it doesn't work

- **Cash drawer** isn't there: only the front desk, managers and owners count the drawer.
- "Count the cash drawer before taking cash today": do the opening count, or ask a manager to skip it.
- "That email and password don't match." or "Only a manager or owner can skip the count.": the skip needs a manager's or owner's own sign-in.
