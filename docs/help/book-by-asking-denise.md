Type what a client asks for in Denise's chat, on any page, and book one of the best times in one tap.

1. Open Denise: tap her face in the bottom right corner (on the calendar, her tab; at checkout, her button at the top), or press **⌘K** or **Ctrl+K**.
2. Type the request the way the client says it, for example "John Williams wants a cut with Gil on a Thursday morning", "earliest balayage with any Senior next week" or "Zoey, same as last time", and send it.
3. If she asks **Which one is it?**, tap the right client. If they aren't on file, check their name and **Mobile** and tap **Add client**.
4. Read the client's alerts on the card: allergies, **Card on file** or **No card on file**, and **Repeat no-show**.
5. Read the best times and why each one suits, then tap **Book** on the one the client wants.
6. Check the service, stylist, time and price, and whether the cancellation policy needs a card, then tap **Confirm booking**. To change anything first, tap **Open in booking form** instead.
7. Not quite right? Just ask: "anything later?", "what about Friday?" or "with Bridget instead?".

## Good to know

- Times come from the same booking rules as every booking, at the length each stylist usually takes for the service where Denise knows it.
- If nothing fits, she shows times on another day, with another stylist at the same level, and **Add to waitlist**.
- On a client's profile, "book a gloss on Friday" means that client.
- If Denise can't read a request, she says why and shows a form (client, service, stylist, day and time of day) that finds times the same way: "I'm offline right now" (she can't reach the AI), "I've reached today's limit" (the salon's daily allowance on Settings is used up; back tomorrow) or "I didn't catch that" (try saying it another way, or use the form). **Book with the form** under her message box opens it any time.
- She never sees the client's name, phone or email, and never contacts them. Stylists and assistants can't book this way.
