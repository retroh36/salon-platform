Take before and after photos of a visit, saved to the client's record.

1. On the calendar, tap the visit to open booking details, then tap the **Photos** tab.
2. Choose **Before** or **After**.
3. Tap **Take photo** to use the camera, or **Upload** to choose photos already on the device.
4. Wait for "Before photo saved." (or "After photo saved.").

## Good to know

- All of a client's photos are on their profile under **Before and after**, grouped by visit, newest first.
- Photos are private to your salon. They're never public and never sent to Denise.
- To remove one, tap the × on it. Only whoever added it, or a manager, can remove a photo.
- To use a photo in marketing, the client's consent must be recorded first (see Record photo consent).
- Photos are made smaller and location details are removed before upload.
