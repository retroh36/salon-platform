Stylists can see what they've earned today, this week and this pay period on My day, with every service and tip.

1. In the sidebar, tap **My day**.
2. Under **Today's earnings**, see what you've earned **Today**, **This week** and **This pay period**, and how far you are from your next tier.
3. Check each service today: its **Price**, any **Back-bar** fee, your **Commission** and the **Tip**. Clients still in your chair show an estimate.
4. Scroll down to **My earnings** for the pay period in detail.
5. Tap **This period** for the pay period so far, or **Last period** for the one before.
6. Check your services, commission, retail, tips and **Total pay**.
7. Tap **My tips** for every tip (date, client, services, tip %, how it was paid), totals by week and day, your average tip and top tippers.
8. Tap **Tickets behind it** to see each ticket that counts toward your pay.

## Good to know

- Commission is on what clients paid before tax, at your rate or plan when each sale was made. Tiers count everything you've sold since they started again.
- An estimate is only a guide; the real figure is set at checkout.
- Refunds come off in the period they happen.
- Your average tip is your tips as a share of your service sales, untipped visits included.
- You only see your own earnings. Ask a manager or owner if something looks wrong.
