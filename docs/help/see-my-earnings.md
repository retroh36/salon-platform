Stylists can see what they've earned this pay period, or the last one, on My day, with every tip.

1. In the sidebar, tap **My day**.
2. Scroll down to **My earnings**.
3. Tap **This period** for the pay period so far, or **Last period** for the one before.
4. Check your services, commission, retail, tips and **Total pay**.
5. Tap **My tips** for every tip (date, client, services, tip %, how it was paid), totals by week and day, your average tip and top tippers.
6. Tap **Tickets behind it** to see each ticket that counts toward your pay.

## Good to know

- Commission is on what clients paid before tax, at your rate when each sale was made.
- Refunds come off in the period they happen.
- Your average tip is your tips as a share of your service sales, untipped visits included.
- You only see your own earnings. Ask a manager or owner if something looks wrong.
