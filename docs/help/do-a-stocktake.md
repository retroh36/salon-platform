Count what's on the shelves, shelf by shelf, and put the stock records right.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Inventory**, then **Stocktake**.
2. Tap **Start a stocktake**.
3. Tap a shelf at the top.
4. For each product, enter the full containers (use **+** and **−**) and, for color and other products by weight, what's left in the **Open one**. Tap **Save count**.
5. Work through each shelf. The bar at the bottom shows how many you've counted.
6. Tap **Review and finish** to see what's different, found or missing.
7. Tap **Finish and update stock**, or **Keep counting**.

## Good to know

- Nothing changes until you finish. Products you didn't count stay as they are.
- Sales and service use during the count aren't held against it.
- Missing stock worth $15 or more, or a fifth of what was expected, is marked **Possible shrinkage**.
- **Cancel stocktake** throws away the counts without changing stock.
