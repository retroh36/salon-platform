Set the hours someone works week after week, the same every week or over 2 to 4 weeks ("Tuesday to Saturday, every other Saturday off"). Future weeks fill in from it.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Scheduling**.
2. Tap the repeat button (two arrows in a loop) next to the person's name.
3. Under **Repeats**, choose **Every week**, or every 2, 3 or 4 weeks.
4. For more than one week, choose which week is **Week 1**.
5. Set each day's shifts. Leave a day empty for a day off.
6. Tap **Save pattern**.

## Good to know

- The pattern changes the weeks ahead straight away and tells the person.
- A day changed on its own in a published week keeps its change.
- If clients are booked outside the new hours, you'll see who, with links to move them, or tap **Save anyway**.
