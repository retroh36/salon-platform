Book a client while they're on the phone: type what they ask for and pick one of the three best times.

1. Go to **Calendar** and tap **Phone booking** (or press **P**).
2. Type what the client asks for the way they say it, for example "Maria Lopez, balayage + cut with Bridget next week, afternoons", "earliest men's cut with anyone" or "same as last time for Zoey", then tap **Find times**. Without Denise, or to skip her, tap **Fill in the fields instead**.
3. Check the client. If Denise found more than one, pick the right one; type a name or phone number in **Find a client**, or tap **New client** to add them.
4. Read the alerts under their name: allergies, **Card on file** or **No card on file**, **Repeat no-show**, and their last visit. Tap **Same as last time** to fill in those services and stylist.
5. Check or change what Denise filled in: **Service** (tap **Add another service** for more, up to 4), **Stylist** (a stylist, a level such as "Any master stylist", or **Anyone**), **From** and **To**, **Time of day**, and **Earliest first**. Tap **Show times** after a change.
6. Under **Best times**, read the three options and why each one suits (their usual stylist or day, right after another client, no gap left).
7. Tap **Book** on the one the client wants. To change anything first (a service, the chair, notes), tap **Adjust** to open it in the booking form.

## Good to know

- Times come from the same booking rules as every booking: the stylist's hours, time off, blocked time, chairs and rooms, and processing time, which another client can use.
- Each stylist is checked at the length they usually take for the service, where Denise knows it, so a slow balayage never runs into the next client.
- If nothing fits, the panel offers **Another day** with the same stylist, **Another stylist at the same level**, and **Add to waitlist** for those dates.
- After booking, the visit opens in **Booking details**, where **Card needed to hold this booking** or **Deposit needed** shows with **Send card link** when the cancellation policy asks for one.
- Denise only reads what you typed to fill in the fields. The client's name, phone and email are taken out before she sees it, and she never contacts the client.
- Stylists and assistants don't have **Phone booking**.
