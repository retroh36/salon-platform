Add a new service to the menu, with its price, timing and who offers it.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Services**, then **Add service**.
2. Enter the **Name**, a **Category** (pick one from the list or type a new one) and the **Default price ($)**.
3. If the service needs a patch test first, tick **Needs a patch test**. If it's done in a room (a facial, a smoothing treatment), tick **Needs a room**.
4. Under **Prices by level**, enter a price for **Artisan**, **Senior** or **Master** if they charge differently. Leave blank to use the default.
5. Under **Time**, enter **Service time (min)**. For color, add a **Processing gap (min)** and any **Finishing time (min)** after it.
6. Under **Who offers this**, tick each stylist who does it. Add a **Price ($)** only for someone with their own price.
7. Tap **Add service**.

## Good to know

- During the processing gap the stylist is free, so another client can be booked in then. The chair stays taken.
- Use 5-minute steps for times.
- To add a product recipe, open the service again after saving. See "Set a service's product recipe".
- Whether a missing patch test only warns or stops the booking is set on the **Forms** page. See "Set patch test rules".
- A service that needs a room books a free room and can't be booked when none is free. Rooms are listed on Settings → Stations.
