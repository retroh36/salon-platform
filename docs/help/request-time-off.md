Ask for time off from your phone or the desk. Managers and owners can also add approved time off for anyone.

1. On **My day**, tap **Ask for time off** next to **My week**. Or go to **Team**, then the **Schedule** tab, and scroll to **Time off**.
2. Choose the **First day**. Set the **Last day**, or leave it blank for one day.
3. For part of a day, tick **Part of the day** and set **From** and **To**.
4. Choose a **Reason** and add a **Note (optional)**.
5. Tap **Request time off** (or **Add time off** if you're a manager or owner).
6. Check **My time off** for the answer: **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Declined** with the reason.

## Good to know

- The salon asks for a set number of days' notice, shown under the form. Asking sooner still works, but it's marked short notice.
- Requests during blackout dates (shown under the form) still go through, flagged for whoever decides.
- Owners and managers get a notice in the app when you ask, and you get one when they decide.
- You can **Withdraw** a request while it's still pending. Only approved time off blocks bookings.
- Time off a manager adds is approved straight away. If a stylist is out unexpectedly today, see "Mark a stylist out".
