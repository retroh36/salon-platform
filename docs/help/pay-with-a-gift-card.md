Use some or all of a gift card's balance to pay at checkout.

1. Open the checkout: tap **Check out** in booking details, or **New sale** on **Sales**.
2. Under **Payment**, tap **Gift card**.
3. If the client has cards on file, tap one. Otherwise scan or type the code and tap **Check**.
4. Check the **Balance** shown. The amount is filled in with what's left to pay, up to the balance; change it if the client wants to use less.
5. Tap **Use $… from the card**.
6. If anything is still left to pay, add another payment (**Card** or **Cash**).
7. When it says **Paid in full**, tap **Complete sale**.

## Good to know

- The payment line shows how much will be left on the card afterwards.
- Gift cards can't be used to buy another gift card or a reload.
- A gift card can't be paid with more than its balance.
- If the sale is later voided, the money goes back on the card.

## If it doesn't work

- "That gift card was voided", "has expired" or "has nothing left on it": the card can't be used. A manager can look at its history on **Gift cards**.
- "No gift card has that code": check the code for typos, or scan it again.
