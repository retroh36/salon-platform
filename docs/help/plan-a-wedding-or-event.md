Set up a wedding or other event on one screen: the details, the party, the timeline, the team and the price.

1. Go to **Events** and tap **New event**.
2. In **Describe it to Denise**, type one line, like "June 14 wedding, ready by 2pm, bride plus 5 bridesmaids hair and makeup, at the Grand Hotel", and tap **Draft it**.
3. Check what she filled in: the **Date**, **Ready by**, **Where** and everyone in the **Party** with their package. Change anything.
4. Tap **+ Bridesmaid**, **+ Mother of the bride** and the other buttons to add someone, or **×** to remove them.
5. Give it a **Name** and find the client under **Find a client** (or tap **New client**).
6. Check the **Total** and the deposit on the side, and the **Timeline** under the party. Change the number of hair or makeup artists, and who's on each, if you need to.
7. Tap **Save as inquiry**.

## Good to know

- You can fill it all in without Denise: skip step 2.
- Nothing is booked, held on the calendar or sent to the client when you save. It's an Inquiry until it's booked.
- The timeline is planned back from the ready-by time: the bride finishes last, everyone has their touch-up at the end, and each artist gets a call time. Drag a bar to change it.
- Denise suggests someone free for each artist. "Has clients booked" or "has time off" next to a name means they aren't free then.
- On-location events add the travel fee, and an artist starting before the early-start time adds the early-start fee.

## If it doesn't work

- **Save as inquiry** is greyed out: the reason is shown just below it.
- Denise couldn't tell who's in the party: say who and what they need, like "bride plus 4 bridesmaids hair".
