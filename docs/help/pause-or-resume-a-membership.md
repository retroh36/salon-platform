Pause a membership for a while, or turn it back on.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Clients** and open the client.
2. Find their membership under **Membership**.
3. To pause, tap **Pause**, choose how long in **Pause for**, then tap **Pause**.
4. To turn a paused membership back on, tap **Resume now**.
5. To cancel a pause that hasn't started yet, tap **Call off the pause**.
6. Check the line under the plan name: it says when it pauses, resumes or renews.

## Good to know

- A pause starts at the end of the period already paid for. There are no payments and no member benefits while it's paused.
- How long it can pause (and whether it can at all) is set on the plan by the owner.
- Resuming starts a new period today and charges the card then. Payments are in test mode for now, so no real card is charged.
- A membership that's set to end can't be paused.
