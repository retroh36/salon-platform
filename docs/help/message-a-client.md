Send a client a text or email about an upcoming visit: a quick pick, or your own words.

1. On the calendar, tap the visit to open booking details.
2. Tap **⋯**, then **Message client**.
3. Under **Message**, choose a quick pick (like **We're running behind** or **Please call us**), or **Write my own**.
4. For your own words, choose what it's about: **Their visit**, or **An offer** (only for clients who said yes to offers).
5. Choose **Text** or **Email**. Only the ways the client gets messages can be chosen.
6. Type your message (and a **Subject** for email). Under a text, the counter shows its length.
7. Tap **Send**.

## Good to know

- Over 160 characters, a text sends as 2 texts (emoji or curly quotes make it 70). The counter warns you.
- "Reply STOP to stop texts." is added to every text, and an unsubscribe link to every offer.
- A client who replied STOP shows **No texts — call or email**; messages go by email instead.
- Every message is saved on the client's profile under **Messages**, with who sent it.
- Denise never writes or sends messages to clients. Texts and emails wait in the outbox until they're connected.

## If it doesn't work

- **Write my own** is missing: the owner has turned it off for your role (Settings → **Messages**). Quick picks still work.
- **Message client** is missing: it's for upcoming visits, from the front desk or a stylist on the visit.
