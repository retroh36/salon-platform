Check a client in when they arrive.

1. On the calendar, tap the client's visit to open booking details.
2. Tap **Check in** at the bottom.
3. If something is still to do (a patch test or a form), a warning lists it. Tap **Check in anyway** or **Not yet** (to sort it out first).
4. The block turns yellow and the status line says "Checked in at…".

## Good to know

- Made a mistake? Tap **Undo** on the message at the bottom of the screen within 10 seconds, or see Undo a check-in.
- Before this visit (with a number) shows above the tabs while a patch test or form is outstanding. Tap it to record the test or tap **Fill it in now**.
- When a stylist taps **Start service** on My day, that checks the client in too.

## If it doesn't work

- "Can't be checked in yet: the salon requires a valid patch test first": record a patch test, or ask a manager or owner. They can type a reason under **Why check in without it?** and tap **Check in without the patch test**.
