Tell Denise a client is running late, or a visit is running over, so she can rearrange the rest of the day.

1. On the calendar, tap the visit to open booking details.
2. Tap **⋯** (More actions) at the bottom, then **Running late**.
3. If the client isn't checked in yet, it asks **How late is** (their name)? If they're checked in, it asks **How far over is this running?**
4. Tap how many minutes (5 to 60 min).
5. For a visit with several services that's running over, choose **Which service is running over?**
6. Tap **Tell Denise**.
7. Denise's panel opens. She works out what it pushes into and suggests fixes to approve (see Approve Denise's fixes).

## Good to know

- Stylists can report this for their own visits; the front desk, managers and owners for any visit.
- It works for today and future days, not for days already over.
- If nothing is affected, Denise says so and nothing changes.
- Clients aren't messaged automatically.
