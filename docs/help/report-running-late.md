Tell Denise a stylist is running late, a visit is running over, or a client is late, to see what it pushes into. Nothing moves until you apply a fix.

1. On the calendar, at the top right of the stylist's column, tap **⋯** (More for their name), then **… is running late…** (for example **Gil is running late…**; **I'm running late…** on your own column). You can also tell Denise ("Gil is running 20 minutes late") and tap the button under the answer.
2. The panel says what it's about: the client in their chair now, or, if nobody's in it yet, their next client.
3. Tap how many minutes (5 to 60 min).
4. With a client in the chair, tap **Show what it pushes into**. Denise's panel opens with the fixes, and where clients would move shows dashed on the calendar.
5. To use a fix, tap **Apply fix**. Then check each moved client's message and tap **Send** (see Approve Denise's fixes). **Undo** puts everyone back.
6. With nobody in the chair yet, tap **Plan for** (the time they'll be in). The **Mark … out** panel opens on **Coming in late…** with Denise's plan for each client (see Mark a stylist out).

For a single client who's late, or one visit running over: tap the visit, then **⋯** (More actions) at the bottom, **Running late**, the minutes and **Tell Denise**.

## Good to know

- Stylists can report this for themselves and their own visits; the front desk, managers and owners for anyone.
- It works for today and future days, not for days already over.
- If nothing is affected, Denise says so and nothing changes.
- Nothing moves until someone applies a fix, and no client is messaged until someone sends their message.
