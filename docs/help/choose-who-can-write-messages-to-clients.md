Choose which roles can write their own messages to clients, as well as the quick picks.

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Messages**.
2. Under **Writing their own messages**, tick or untick **Managers**, **Front desk** and **Stylists**.
3. Each change saves by itself.

## Good to know

- It's on for managers, the front desk and stylists until you change it. Owners can always write their own.
- With it off, that role can still send the quick picks from **Message client**.
- Stylists only message clients on their own visits.
- Whoever writes it, a message only goes the ways the client allows, and a STOP is always honored. Denise never writes to clients.
