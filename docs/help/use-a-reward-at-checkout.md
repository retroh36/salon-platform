Take a reward off a client's sale with their points, in one tap.

1. Open the checkout: tap **Check out** in booking details, or **New sale** on **Sales** and find the client.
2. Next to the client's name, check their points and tier. Denise says when they're close to a reward.
3. Under the lines, find **Rewards**. Rewards they can take now can be tapped. The others say why not yet.
4. Tap the reward. It shows on the ticket as **Reward** with what it takes off.
5. If their tier has a free add-on, tap it under **Rewards** too.
6. Take payment and tap **Complete sale**. The points come off, and the points for this sale go on.

## Good to know

- A free service reward takes the first line for that service, in full. Add the service to the ticket first.
- Rewards don't go with a promotion unless the salon allows it.
- To take a reward back off before completing, tap the × next to it.
- If the sale is voided later, the reward's points go back to the client.

## If it doesn't work

- The reward is greyed out: the reason is under it, like "40 more points needed" or "Already used this year".
- No **Rewards** at all: the client isn't on the sale, or the rewards program is off.
