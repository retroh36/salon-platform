Take a promotion off a visit or sale: a code the client gives, or an offer that applies here.

1. Open the checkout for the visit or sale.
2. Under the lines, tap **Promotion**.
3. Type the client's code in **Promo code** and tap **Apply code**, or tap **Apply** next to an offer under **Offers**.
4. Check the promotion under the lines, with what it takes off, and the **Discounts** line in the totals.
5. To take one off, tap the × next to it.
6. Take payment and tap **Complete sale**.

## Good to know

- A code or special the client used when booking online, and automatic offers that apply, are already on the ticket when the checkout opens.
- Each promotion's rules are checked: its dates, how many times it can be used, new clients or first visits only, and a minimum spend.
- Promotions don't count against your discount limit, and every use is recorded with who completed the sale.
- An offer that can't be used here says why instead of **Apply**.

## If it doesn't work

- "… can't be used with another discount": remove the line or sale discount, or the promotion.
- "No promotion has that code, or it has ended": check the spelling with the client.
