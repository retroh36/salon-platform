Save a view of just the stylists you need, in the order you want, for example "Hair team", "Color" or "My section".

1. Go to **Calendar**.
2. Open the department menu (it says **All departments**) and tap **New view…**.
3. Type a **Name**.
4. Under **Who's in it**, tick the stylists. Tap a department button (for example **+ Nails**) to add everyone in it, or type in **Find a stylist**.
5. Under **In this order**, drag a stylist by the dots or use the arrows. The top of the list is the left of the calendar.
6. Tap **Save view**. The calendar shows only those stylists.
7. To change it later, pick it in the menu, open the menu again and tap **Edit** with its name. You can also **Delete view** there.

## Good to know

- Your views are yours: only you see them. Owners can tick **Share with everyone at the salon** to make a view for the whole team. Shared views are listed under **Views for everyone**.
- Only owners and managers can change or delete a shared view.
- The calendar remembers the last view you used on each device.
- To go back to everyone, pick **All departments**. Departments (Color, Cuts, Nails, Skin…) show everyone who does those services.
- Stylists who are off still don't show in a view. See See who's off today.
