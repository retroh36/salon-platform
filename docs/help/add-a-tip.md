Add a tip for each stylist who did a service on the ticket.

1. Open the checkout for the visit.
2. On the right, find **Tips · on each stylist's services**. There's a row for each stylist with services on the ticket.
3. Tap **18%**, **20%** or **25%** (worked out on that stylist's services), or tap **Custom** and type the amount.
4. Tap **None** to take a tip off again.
5. The **Tips** line and the **Total** update. Take payment for the new total, then tap **Complete sale**.

## Good to know

- Each stylist's tip is kept separately, so it goes to the right person in payroll.
- Tips are part of the total, so the payments need to cover them.
- If the client pays by payment link, they choose their own tips on their phone.
- Tips only show for stylists with a service on the ticket; to tip someone else, add their service first.
