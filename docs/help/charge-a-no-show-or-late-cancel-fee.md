Fees charge themselves: mark the no-show or cancel the visit as usual, and the policy does the rest.

1. Open the visit in booking details and tap **⋯**.
2. Tap **Mark no-show** (or **Cancel booking** and pick the client's reason).
3. Check the fee shown, then confirm.
4. About 10 minutes later the fee is charged to the card on file, and the client gets a receipt.
5. If it didn't go through, open the visit or the client's profile and tap **Try the card again**, or send them a card link.

## Good to know

- Only late cancellations by the client cost anything. Cancellations by the salon don't.
- Undo the no-show within 10 minutes and nothing is charged.
- A deposit paid ahead counts toward the fee.
- Fees that didn't go through are listed on the Dashboard under **Fees that didn't go through**.
