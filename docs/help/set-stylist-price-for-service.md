Choose which stylists offer a service, and give one of them their own price for it.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Services**.
2. Tap the service.
3. Scroll to **Who offers this**.
4. Tick each stylist who offers the service.
5. To give a stylist their own price, enter it in their **Price ($)** box. Leave it blank to use their level's price, or the default price.
6. Tap **Save changes**.

## Good to know

- The price used is the stylist's own price, else their level's price, else the service's default price.
- The empty box shows which price they'd charge without one, for example "Senior $95".
- Only stylists ticked here can be booked for the service, on the calendar and online.
