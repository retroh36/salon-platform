Book the bride's trial from the event, at a time the booking rules say is free.

1. Go to **Events** and tap the booked event.
2. Under **Trial**, choose the **Trial day**. It starts about a month before the event.
3. Tap **Find times**.
4. Tap a time. The event's hair artists come first.

## Good to know

- The trial is an ordinary visit on the calendar, for the event's client, with the salon's trial service.
- When the trial is checked out, the event moves to **Trial done** on the board.
- The trial service is set on **Settings → Bridal and events**.
- Write down what you decided under **The look** on the event, so the team has it on the day.

## If it doesn't work

- "Nobody's free that day": try another day.
- No **Trial** card: the event has to be booked first.
