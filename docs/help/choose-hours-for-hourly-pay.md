Pay people paid by the hour for their scheduled hours or the hours they clocked.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Settings**, then **Payroll**.
2. Under **Hours for hourly pay**, choose **Scheduled hours** or **Clocked hours**.
3. Tap **Save hours**.

## Good to know

- Scheduled hours are the default: their shifts on the published schedule, less approved time off.
- **Clocked hours** needs the time clock on (Settings → Shared devices, "The team clocks in and out").
- Payroll's **Hours** section shows scheduled hours, clocked hours and the difference for everyone paid by the hour, whichever you choose.
- The choice applies to every period you open, including ones already over, so check a period before and after if you've already paid it.
- Only owners change it. Every change is kept with who made it, on the settings page and in the Audit trail (Pay rates and payroll hours).
