Choose the preset amounts staff tap at checkout and how long cards last.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Settings**, then **Gift cards**.
2. In **Preset amounts ($, separated by commas)**, type the amounts you want as quick buttons (for example 50, 100, 150).
3. In **Bought cards expire after (months)**, enter a number, or leave it empty for no expiry.
4. In **Promotional cards last (days)**, enter how long free cards last by default.
5. Tap **Save**.

## Good to know

- Gift card laws (how long cards must last, fees, unused balances) vary by state. Check your local law before setting an expiry.
- Changes apply to cards sold or issued from now on.
- Staff can still type any amount from $5 to $1,000 at checkout.
