Each role starts with what it needs; an owner can switch extra things on or off for one person.

1. In the sidebar, under **Salon**, tap **Team**.
2. Tap the person's name.
3. Under **What they can do**, choose their **Permission group** if you use groups.
4. Tick or clear each switch for just them: **Salon settings**, **Manage the team**, **Payroll and pay rates**, **Reports and past sales**, **Download the salon's data**, **Void and refund sales**, **Waive cancellation fees**, **Give discounts over the limit**, **See other stylists' numbers**, **Adjust rewards points** or **See the audit trail**.
5. It saves as you tick. "(changed for …)" shows a switch changed for just them; tap **back to …** to undo it. "(from …)" shows one their group gives them.

## Good to know

- Owners can do everything. Managers can do everything but billing (the plan and Denise's limit) and deleting the business, and can't manage owners.
- The front desk gets the calendar, check-in and checkout, clients and gift cards, and today's sales only. Stylists get their own day, clients, tips, pay and goals. Assistants see the day of the stylist they assist.
- Only what makes sense for the role is listed: a stylist can be allowed to see other stylists' numbers, but never payroll.
- **Adjust rewards points** is for managers by default; an owner can give it to the front desk.
- A switch changed for one person wins over their group. To set the same switches for several people, use a group: see "Set up permission groups".
- It applies the next time they open a page.
- Anyone can see what they're allowed on **Your account** (tap your name at the bottom of the sidebar).
