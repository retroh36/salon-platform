Clients can have their own account on the salon's booking site, to see and change their visits, check balances, manage their card and choose their messages.

1. Tell them to open the salon's booking page and tap **My account** at the top.
2. They choose **Email** or **Text**, enter it and tap **Send my code**.
3. They enter the 6-digit code and tap **Sign in**, or tap the link in the message. There's no password.
4. If you have their email or phone on file, they're matched to their profile. If not, they give their name to create one.
5. In their account they see **Coming up** (with **Change or cancel**), **Balances**, **Card on file**, **Messages** and **Past visits**, and **Book again** for their last visit.

## Good to know

- When booking online, ticking **Keep me signed in on this device** creates their account too. They can always book without one.
- **Change or cancel** shows the cancellation policy and any fee before anything changes, and only until the salon's cutoff.
- Cards are kept by the payment provider; nobody sees the number. Test cards only for now.
- A client's profile says "Has an online account" once they've made one.
- Under **Messages** they choose texts and emails **About your visits** and **Offers and news** separately; every change shows in **Consent history** on their profile.
- Codes go to the outbox for now, until texts and email are connected.
- **Rewards** shows their points, tier and rewards, and **Copy my referral link** gives them a link to share: both earn points once the friend's first visit is done.

## If it doesn't work

- "Too many sign-in tries": they can try again in an hour, or you can help them at the desk.
- Their code or link expired: codes and links last 10 minutes and work once. They can ask for a new one.
