See what each product went through over any dates: used in services and sold, what's on hand and on order, and what to order.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Inventory**, then **Usage**.
2. Under **From** and **To**, choose the dates (the last 30 days to start), then tap **Show**.
3. Read Denise's note at the top: color, back bar and retail against the same dates last year, and the products that changed most.
4. Check each product's **Used in services**, **Sold**, **On hand**, **On order** and **Suggested order**.

## Good to know

- **Used in services** is what checkout took off for formulas and recipes; **Sold** is retail sold, less anything returned to stock.
- Until there's a year of history, Denise compares with the same number of days just before (the column says **Period before**).
- A **Suggested order** covers another stretch like the one shown and keeps the product above its reorder point, never less than its **Order at least**. Make the order under **Orders**. See "Create and send a purchase order".
- Stocktake counts and corrections aren't usage, so they're left out.
