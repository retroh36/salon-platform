Download everything your salon keeps here as spreadsheet files (CSV) in one zip.

1. Go to **Settings** and tap **Your data**.
2. Check **What's included**: clients, visits, formulas, sales, products, gift cards, packages, services and team.
3. Tap **Download all data**.
4. Save the zip file. Open the files in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.
5. Check **Downloads** below: each download is listed with who made it and when.

## Good to know

- You can download your data any time; you never need permission.
- Card numbers are never stored, so they're never in it.
- Before and after photos aren't in the zip; they stay in your private storage.
- The file has clients' private details (phones, notes, allergies): keep it somewhere safe.
- Only owners can download, and every download is logged.
