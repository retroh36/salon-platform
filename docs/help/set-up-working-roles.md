A working role is what someone is doing in a stretch of their day, like Wash House in the morning and Beauty Bar in the afternoon. Each role sets the services that can be booked then, whether that time shows on the booking site, and how it's paid.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Settings**, then **Working roles**.
2. Tap **Add a role**.
3. Type its **Name**.
4. Leave **Clients can book this time online** ticked, or untick it to keep that time off the booking site.
5. Under **Services**, choose **All their services**, or **Only these** and tick the services that can be booked in this role.
6. Under **Pay**, choose how this time is paid: **Their usual pay**, **Hourly** (with the rate) or **Commission** (with the percent).
7. Tap **Save role**.
8. To choose who works it, open **Team**, tap the person's name, and tick their roles under **Working roles**.
9. To give a shift its role, open **Scheduling**, tap the shift, choose its role, and publish the week as usual. A repeating pattern's shifts can have roles too.

## Good to know

- Without roles, everyone works as they always have: all their services, online, at their usual pay.
- Only people who run payroll see and change **Pay**. Someone can have their own rate in a role, set by payroll.
- An hourly role pays each hour at the rate and no commission on its services. A commission role pays its services at the percent and not its hours.
- While someone is on a shift in a role, only the role's services can be booked with them, at the desk, online and by Denise. A role that isn't online keeps that time off the booking site; the desk can still book it.
- Dragging a visit onto someone in a role without that service asks first, like any other move that doesn't fit.
- **Remove role** takes it out of use: shifts that had it book as if they had no role.
- Payroll pays each shift as its role pays, with a **By working role** table. Each service records the role it was done in when it's sold, and a change to a role's pay counts from then on.
- Taking someone off a role takes it off their shifts still to come.
