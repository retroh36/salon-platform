Mark a visit as a no-show when the client doesn't come.

1. On the calendar, tap the visit to open booking details.
2. Tap **⋯** (More actions) at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mark no-show**. It's done straight away.

## Good to know

- Or, from Denise's panel: a client 15 minutes late shows under **Not arrived yet**. Tap **Mark no-show** in their row.
- Only visits that are booked or confirmed can be marked as no-shows (not once the client is checked in).
- On the calendar, a no-show turns grey with the name struck through.
- The time it frees up may show in Denise's panel with waitlist clients who could take it.
- If the client is on the way, don't mark a no-show: use Report running late instead.
