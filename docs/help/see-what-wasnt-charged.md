In dollars, for any dates: what no-shows and late cancellations were booked for, what the policy collected, and why the rest wasn't charged.

1. In the sidebar, under Sales, tap **Policy**, then **Not charged, and why** under the totals.
2. Choose the **From** and **To** dates (up to a year), then tap **Show**.
3. Read the totals: **Booked value**, **Collected**, **Not charged** and **Fees not collected**.
4. Under **Why**, each reason with its visits and dollars: **No card on file**, **Card declined**, **Waived** (with each reason staff gave), **Waiting to be charged**, **The policy sets no fee** and **No fee assessed**.

## Good to know

- Visits count on the day they were booked for. A cancellation in good time, or one the salon made, isn't a late cancellation.
- **Not charged** is the booked value of visits with no fee collected. A fee the policy set below the visit's price counts as charged once it's collected.
- **No card on file** fees charge by themselves once the client adds a card. **Card declined** ones can be tried again from the fee on **Policy**.
- To charge more of them, change the fees or ask for a card on more bookings in Settings, **Policies**.
- No client names: a bookkeeper sees the same totals.
