Choose what clients see when they book online: a cover photo and a short description of the salon, each stylist's portrait, specialty, bio and photos of their work, and a description under each service.

1. Go to **Settings** and tap **Booking page**.
2. Under **Cover and description**, tap **Upload** to add a **Cover photo** (a wide photo of the salon works best).
3. Write a **Short description** of the salon and tap **Save description**.
4. Under **Stylists**, tap **Upload** under a stylist's **Portrait**.
5. Write their **Specialty** (one line, like "Lived-in color and balayage") and, if you like, a **Bio**, then tap their Save button (**Save Bridget's profile** for Bridget).
6. Under **Their work**, tap **Upload** to add up to 3 photos of their work.
7. Under **Category photos**, tap **Upload** to add a photo above a part of your menu.
8. Under **Service descriptions**, write a short line for each service and tap **Save descriptions**.
9. Tap **View booking page** to see it as clients do.

## Good to know

- The booking site shows the salon's address with a map link, its phone number and opening hours from your salon's details, so there's nothing to add for those.
- Without a portrait, the stylist's initials show. Without a cover photo, the page starts with your logo and name.
- Stylists show with their full name, level and specialty. Clients tap **About** to read the bio and see the photos.
- Photos are made smaller before they upload, and the location and camera details are taken out.
- A client signed in to their account sees **Book again** at the top, with their last visit's services and stylist.
- Everything here is public on your booking site.
- Service descriptions are the same ones you edit on Services.

## If it doesn't work

- **Use a photo up to 2 MB** or **Use a JPEG, PNG or WebP photo**: try another photo.
- Only owners, and managers who manage the team, can change stylists' profiles.
