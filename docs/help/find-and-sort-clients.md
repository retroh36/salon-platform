The Clients list shows each client's last visit, next visit, usual stylist and spend, and sorts by any of them.

1. Go to **Clients**.
2. Type a name or part of a phone number in the search box. The list narrows as you type.
3. Tap a column heading (**Name**, **Last visit**, **Next visit**, **Usual stylist** or **Spend**) to sort by it.
4. Tap the same heading again to sort the other way. The arrow shows which way.
5. Tap a client's name to open their profile.

## Good to know

- **Last visit** is the last one they were checked out of. **Next visit** is their next booking that isn't over yet.
- **Usual stylist** and **Spend** come from what Denise has learned, updated each night and after every checkout.
- Sorting keeps your search, and searching keeps your sort.
- Stylists and assistants see only the clients on their own book, with no **Spend** column.
- The list shows 50 at a time. Search to narrow it down.
