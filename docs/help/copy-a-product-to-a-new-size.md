Add a product you already stock in another size, without typing it all again.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Inventory**, then tap the product.
2. Tap **Copy to a new size**.
3. **Add another size** opens with the brand, line, name, type, supplier and shelf filled in.
4. Enter the new **Container size** and **Cost per container ($)** (and the **Retail price ($)** for retail).
5. Add its **SKU** and **Barcode** if it has them.
6. Tap **Add product**.

## Good to know

- Retail products are counted in units, so put the size in the name (like "Shampoo 1 L") to tell them apart.
- The copy is a product of its own: it starts with nothing on hand and its own reorder settings. See "Set when to reorder a product".
- Each size needs its own SKU and barcode; two products can't share one.
