Drag a visit on the calendar to a new time or another stylist's column.

1. Go to **Calendar** and find the visit.
2. Press on the block and drag it up or down to change the time (no need to click it first; it moves in 5-minute steps). On the iPad, hold your finger still on it for a moment until it lifts, then drag. While you drag, a preview shows exactly where it will land, with the time and stylist ("6:05–6:35pm · Gil").
3. To give it to another stylist, drag it sideways into their column. Any stylist's column takes it.
4. Let go. If nothing's in the way, the visit moves straight away.
5. If something is in the way, the preview turns amber while you drag and says why, and letting go asks first: "Gil doesn't usually do Balayage. Move anyway?", "Gil has Lillian then. Double-book anyway?", or "Outside Gil's hours. Move anyway?". Tap the button to move it anyway, or **Cancel** to leave it where it was.
6. At the top of the calendar, **Let … know about the new time?** shows the message. Tap **Send** to send it, or **Don't send**.
7. If it's a repeating visit or part of a group, choose what to move: **This visit only**, **The whole group**, **This and following visits** or **All visits in the series** (or **Cancel**).

## Good to know

- Only visits that are booked, confirmed or checked in can be moved.
- A simple click (without dragging) opens booking details as usual.
- On the iPad, a quick swipe scrolls the calendar, even if it starts on a visit. Only a press held still picks a visit up.
- Hold a visit near the top or bottom of the calendar (or a side) and the day scrolls under it.
- Another client in a color's processing time is fine: it drops without asking.
- A visit moved anyway onto a stylist who doesn't usually do the service takes their level's price for it. A double-booking you confirmed shows up in Denise's panel, in case you'd like her to sort it out.
- Dragging stays on the same day. To move to another day, see Move an appointment to another day.
- The stylists get a notice. Nothing goes to the client unless you tap **Send** (messages wait in the outbox until texts are connected). A client who takes no messages shows **No messages: call** instead.
- Busy day? Tick **Don't ask again today** and the calendar stops asking on this device until tomorrow. Clients moved then aren't told; use **Message client** in booking details.
- Every move is checked against the booking rules (stylist hours, double-booking, chairs, blocked time) first; you're asked before anything breaks one.
- Moving a whole group shifts everyone by the same amount.
- To change services or the exact time, use Edit an appointment.

## If it doesn't work

- The block snaps back: the move couldn't be saved (a service that needs a room with none free, say, or someone changed the calendar at the same moment). The reason shows in a red message at the top of the calendar, which goes by itself after 3 seconds (it waits while the pointer is over it; × closes it).
- Moving a repeating visit's following visits, or a whole group, never double-books: if one doesn't fit, nothing moves.
