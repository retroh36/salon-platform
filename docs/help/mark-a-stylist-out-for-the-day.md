When a stylist can't work (sick, an emergency), mark them out and Denise finds new places for their clients.

1. Go to **Calendar** and open the day.
2. At the top right of the stylist's column, tap **⋯** (More for their name).
3. To see every client at once and move them together, tap **Plan the rescue** (see Plan a sick-day rescue). Or tap **Out for the day** (today it says **Out for the rest of the day**).
4. After **Out for the day**, Denise's panel opens with her suggested fixes, one client at a time: moving clients to other stylists or times. Review and apply them (see Approve Denise's fixes).

## Good to know

- It also adds approved time off for the stylist, so nothing else can be booked with them.
- Stylists can mark only themselves out (only **Out for the day**); the front desk, managers and owners can mark anyone.
- To mark someone out from a time (they have to leave at 1), use **Plan the rescue**.
- Only the front desk, managers and owners can apply the fixes.
- Some clients may have no fix. Denise lists them so you can call and rebook.
- Clients aren't messaged automatically; get in touch with them yourself.
