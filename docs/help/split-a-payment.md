Take payment in more than one way, such as part cash and part card.

1. Open the checkout and check the **Total**.
2. Under **Payment**, tap the first way, for example **Card**.
3. Change the **Amount ($)** to what's going on this one, and tap **Record … card payment**.
4. The box below shows what's still **remaining to pay**.
5. Tap the next way, for example **Cash**. Enter what's handed over (or tap **Exact $…**) and tap **Record … cash**.
6. Repeat until it says **Paid in full**, then tap **Complete sale**.

## Good to know

- Each payment shows as a line under **Payment**. Tap its **×** to remove one you got wrong.
- Cash shows the change to give, both on the cash screen and after the sale.
- **Gift card** and **Package** can be mixed with card and cash too.
- Card payments are taken on the salon's own terminal; you only record them here.

## If it doesn't work

- "The payments are $… more than the total.": remove a payment and record it again with the right amount.
