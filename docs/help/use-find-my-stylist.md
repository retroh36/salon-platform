New clients answer a few questions on your booking site and get a suggested service and stylists.

1. To turn it on, go to **Settings** and tap **Online booking**, then tick **Show Find my stylist on the booking site** and save.
2. Clients open **Find my stylist** at the top of your booking site.
3. When they book from their match, open the visit on the calendar. Their answers and photos are in booking details under **From Find my stylist**.
4. If it says the visit may need more time, tap **Add** to lengthen it, or **Ignore**.

## Good to know

- The match is worked out by the salon's own rules: what each stylist's specialty says, level for bigger changes, price if they asked, and who has time in the next two weeks.
- A complete color change over box dye or a chemical treatment suggests a consultation first, if you've set a consultation service.
- Photos are seen only by the salon's team. They are never sent to Denise's AI service.
- Add a specialty for each stylist on **Settings** → **Booking page** so the match can use it.

## If it doesn't work

- **Add** says the time doesn't fit: someone is booked straight after. Move that visit, or tap **Ignore**.
