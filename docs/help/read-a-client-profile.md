A client's profile shows what matters first: allergies, who they are, their notes, then their history.

1. Go to **Clients** and tap the client's name.
2. At the top: any allergies (in red), then their name, phone and email (tap to call or email), their stylist, when they became a client and their tags.
3. Under their name: **Book**, **Message**, **Check out** and **Edit details**.
4. Read their **Notes**, then Denise's summary of their habits.
5. Tap a tab for their history: **Visits** (today first, then upcoming, then past, with the total and tip), **Formulas**, **Photos**, **Patch tests** or **Forms**.
6. Beside it (below it on a narrow screen), a card each: **At a glance** (completed visits, total spend, average ticket and next visit, then last visit), **Details** (with **Edit**), **Packages**, **Membership**, **Rewards**, **Card on file**, **Cancellation policy** and **Messages**.

## Good to know

- Tags: **VIP** is the salon's top tenth by spend, **New** is before their first visit and for 60 days after, **Repeat no-show** is 2 or more no-shows in a year.
- The tab you used last opens next time, on this device.
- Tap a visit's date to open it on the calendar.
- A visit today, even one in the chair past its booked time, is under **Today**.
- The **Visits** tab counts every visit, upcoming and cancelled ones too, and says how they add up ("9 visits: 4 completed, 3 no-shows, …"). **At a glance** counts only **Completed visits**.
- The cards are in the same order for everyone; a card you can't see is left out.
- Stylists and assistants see the same page for their own clients, without money or editing.
- **Message** is about their next visit, so it works once they have one booked.
- **Rewards** (front desk, managers and owners, when the program is on) shows their points and tier, how far to the next tier, the rewards they can take and their referral code (**Copy**). **View history** shows every change to their points.
