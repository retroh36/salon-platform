Choose how often you run payroll, so Payroll and each stylist's earnings show the right dates.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Settings**, then **Payroll**.
2. Under **Pay period**, choose **Weekly**, **Every two weeks**, **Twice a month (1st–15th, 16th–end)** or **Custom (pick the dates each time)**.
3. For weekly or every two weeks, enter the **First day of any pay period**. Any past or future first day works; the rest follow from it.
4. Tap **Save**.

## Good to know

- Owners and managers with payroll can change the pay period.
- With **Custom**, pick the dates on the Payroll page each time.
- Commission and hourly rates are set per person on Team → Schedule. See "Set someone's commission or hourly rate".
