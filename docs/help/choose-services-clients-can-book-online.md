Pick, service by service, what clients can book themselves, what needs a consultation first, and any deposit.

1. Go to **Settings** and tap **Online booking**.
2. Scroll to **Services online**.
3. For each service, choose **Bookable online**, **Consultation first** or **Not online**.
4. To ask for a deposit, choose **Fixed ($)** or **Percent (%)** next to the service, then type the amount.
5. Leave **No deposit** for services that don't need one.
6. Tap **Save services**.

## Good to know

- **Consultation first** services show a **Request a consultation** button on the booking site. It books the service you chose under **"Request a consultation" books** in **Rules and look**; if that's set to nothing, clients are asked to call.
- The same deposit is asked for when the desk books the service: booking details says **Deposit needed** with a link to send.
- Clients pay the deposit online when they book (test cards for now: no real money moves yet). It counts toward checkout, and toward the fee if they cancel late or don't show.
- Services that need a patch test say "New clients need a patch test 48 hours before color" on the booking site. Clients are asked at that step whether they've had one; without one, they book the patch test first (if **Patch tests are done at** is set on **Rules and look**), or are asked to call. A client who says yes but has none on file is told as soon as they confirm their code, before **Review and book**.
- Clients see a price range across stylists until they pick one (or a time, with No preference), using each stylist's own or level price; from then on the price is exact.
