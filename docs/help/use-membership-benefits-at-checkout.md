Members get their included services and member prices at checkout, mostly by themselves.

1. In booking details, tap **Check out**.
2. Look at the badge next to the client's name: their membership and what's left this period.
3. Check the service lines: an included service is already ticked and says **included in** the membership.
4. If a service wasn't ticked (for example one added at checkout), tap **Membership** under **Payment** and tap the service.
5. To pay for a service normally instead, tap the line and tap **Don't use the membership for this**.
6. Member prices come off by themselves: lines say **member price** with the percent off.
7. Take payment for the rest, then tap **Complete sale**.

## Good to know

- Included services aren't taxed. One credit covers one service.
- Member prices come off before any staff discount and don't count against discount limits.
- A paused membership, or one whose payment failed, gives no benefits until it's back on or paid.
- If the sale is voided, included services go back on the membership.

## If it doesn't work

- **Membership** is greyed out: nothing on the ticket is included this period, or it's all used.
