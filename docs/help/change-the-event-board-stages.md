The columns on the **Events** board are yours to name and order, and you can add your own, up to 12.

1. Go to **Settings** and open **Bridal and events**.
2. Under **Board stages**, tap **Rename** to change a name, and drag a stage or use its arrows to move it.
3. Tap **Add stage** and type a name to add your own.
4. Tap **Save stages**.
5. To put an event in a stage of your own, open the event and choose it under **Stage**.

## Good to know

- The six built-in stages can be renamed and moved but not removed, because events move through them by themselves.
- Choosing a stage never changes an event's payments or contract.
- When an event's status changes, or its day comes, it leaves your stage and goes where its status puts it.
- Removing a stage of your own sends its events back to where their status puts them.
