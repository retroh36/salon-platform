You can set your own goals on My day. Denise tells you how you're pacing and what you need each working day to get there.

1. In the sidebar, tap **My day**.
2. Scroll down to **My goals** and tap **Set a goal**.
3. Choose the **Goal** (Service revenue, Retail sales, Rebooking, New clients or Average ticket) and **Each** (week, month, quarter or year).
4. Enter the **Target** and tap **Save goal**.
5. Each goal shows how far along you are, whether you're **Ahead**, **On pace** or **Behind**, how much you need per working day, how many working days are left and what's already booked.
6. Under **Denise's goal check-ins**, choose **Daily** or **Weekly**. Tap **Got it** on her note to put it away until the next one.

## Good to know

- Working days come from your schedule, less time off, so a day off doesn't count against you.
- Denise celebrates when you're halfway, three quarters of the way and when you hit a goal.
- Her ideas come from your own book: regulars due back, open gaps this week and clients who usually buy retail.
- Setting the same goal and period again changes the target.
- A goal the owner set says **Set by the owner**. Only an owner can change or remove it.
- Your goals also show on your Dashboard.
