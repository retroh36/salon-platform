You can set your own goals on My day. Denise tells you how you're pacing and what you need each working day to get there.

1. In the sidebar, tap **My day**.
2. Scroll down to **My goals** and tap **Set a goal**.
3. Choose the **Goal** (Service revenue, Retail sales, Rebooked at checkout, New clients or Average ticket) and **Each** (week, month, quarter or year).
4. Enter the **Target** and tap **Save goal**.
5. Each goal shows how far along you are, whether you're **Ahead**, **On pace** or **Behind**, how much you need per working day, how many working days are left and what's already booked.
6. Denise's note at the top sums your goals up in one line ("On pace for service revenue · behind on rebooking") with her ideas under it; what each goal needs is in its own card. Under **Denise's goal check-ins**, choose **Daily** or **Weekly**. Tap **Got it** on the note to put it away until the next one.

## Good to know

- Working days come from your schedule, less time off, so a day off doesn't count against you.
- Denise celebrates when you're halfway, three quarters of the way and when you hit a goal.
- Denise's ideas come from your own book: regulars due back, open gaps this week and clients who usually buy retail.
- Setting the same goal and period again changes the target.
- A goal the owner set says **Set by the owner**. Only an owner can change or remove it.
- **Rebooked at checkout** counts this period's checkouts where the next visit was booked before they paid. The Dashboard's **Rebooked before leaving** is Denise's figure over the past 12 months, counting a next visit booked any way by the end of the next day, so the two can differ.
- Your goals also show on your Dashboard.
