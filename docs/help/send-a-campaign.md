A campaign is one message, in your own words, to a list of clients from a client report.

1. Under **Clients**, tap **Client reports** and build the list, or ask Denise for it and tap **Open the report**.
2. Tap **Send a campaign**.
3. Give it a **Name**, choose **Send by**, and write the **Email subject** and **Message**. Check the preview.
4. Tap **Send to**, then **Yes, send it**.

## Good to know

- Only clients who said yes to offers get it, on a channel they allow, and each message has a way to opt out.
- Anyone who had an offer or reminder from the salon in the last week is left out. The page says how many.
- Campaigns go out between 9am and 8pm at the salon.
- Denise can build the list, but she never writes or sends the message.
- Under **Sales**, **Campaigns** shows each one: who got it, how many booked within two weeks, and who opted out.
- Until texts and email are connected, messages wait in the outbox.

## If it doesn't work

- **Writing your own messages is turned off for your role:** an owner can change that on **Settings** → **Messages**.
